Clear expectations

What our nurses do, and don’t do

Being clear from the start keeps everyone happy, and keeps mother and baby safe.

Your nurse will

  • Care for baby: feeds, nappies, bathing, cord and skin care, settling
  • Support mum’s recovery, rest and comfort, and help with feeding
  • Follow your doctor’s or midwife’s written instructions
  • Watch for warning signs and tell you straight away
  • Wash and sterilise bottles, do baby’s laundry, keep the nursery tidy
  • Keep written notes and hand over at every change of shift

Your nurse won’t

  • Replace your doctor, midwife or hospital check-ups
  • Prescribe or change medicines
  • Deliver babies at home
  • Do general housework, cooking or laundry for the whole family
  • Care for older children, unless agreed in your plan
  • Share anything about your family with others

Good to know

A few things we ask of families

  • Live-in care: a safe, private place to sleep, meals during the stay and agreed rest each day.
  • Night care: a chair or bed near the cot and a light the nurse can use.
  • Changes: 24 hours’ notice to move or cancel a shift, please.
  • Emergencies: your hospital and doctor’s numbers, kept where the nurse can see them.
  • Family abroad can book and pay for a loved one in Kampala.
