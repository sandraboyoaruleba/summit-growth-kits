Peace of mind

Someone you can trust in your home

Inviting a nurse into your home, at the most tender time of your life, takes trust. Here is how we earn it.

  • Trained nurses and midwives. Ask to see your nurse’s certificate and practising licence.
  • You meet before care starts. In person or by video call, so you know who is coming.
  • Hygiene first. Hand-washing, clean uniforms and careful bottle sterilising.
  • Written notes. Feeds, nappies, sleep and observations, shared at every handover.
  • We know our limits. We never replace your doctor; if something worries us, we tell you at once.

Who are your nurses, and are they qualified?

Our care team are trained nurses and midwives, and every nurse holds a current practising licence. Before care starts we tell you who is coming, and you are welcome to see their certificates and licence. Rachael Mugenyi leads the team and stays in touch with you throughout.

How much does home care cost?

Our rates depend on the level of care and the hours of service tailored to your need. As a guide: home visits from UGX 60,000 (≈ US$16), day care from UGX 70,000 a day (≈ US$19), night care from UGX 90,000 a night (≈ US$24) and live-in care from UGX 550,000 a week (≈ US$150). These are sample starting rates for this preview. See plans & fees.

What does the nurse do, and what don’t they do?

Your nurse cares for mother and baby: feeding support, bathing, cord and skin care, settling and safe sleep, comfort care after birth, watching for warning signs, and keeping written notes. They also keep baby’s things in order: bottles sterilised, baby laundry, a tidy nursery.

They don’t replace your doctor or midwife, don’t prescribe medicines, don’t deliver babies at home, and are not housekeepers for the whole household. If anything worries them, they tell you straight away and help you get to a health facility.

When should I book?

Ideally from around 30–34 weeks of pregnancy, so we can meet you, plan the hours and have your nurse ready when baby arrives. We also help families at short notice after birth: message us and we will tell you honestly what we can arrange.
