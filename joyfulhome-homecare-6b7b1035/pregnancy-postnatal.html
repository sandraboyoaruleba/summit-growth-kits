What to expect

A postnatal visit, step by step

  1. 0:00

    Hello and hand-washing

    Your nurse arrives on time, in uniform, and washes their hands before touching you or baby.

  2. 0:10

    How are you?

    Rest, pain, bleeding, mood, eating and drinking. Mum comes first.

  3. 0:30

    Baby from head to toe

    Feeding, nappies, skin, cord, sleep and settling, with tips you can use tonight.

  4. 1:00

    Feeding together

    We watch a feed and help with latch, position, or bottles and sterilising.

  5. 1:45

    Your plan, written down

    What to keep doing, what to watch for, and when to call your doctor.

Your safety

When to go to hospital straight away

Our nurses support, never replace, your doctor or midwife. Whether or not a nurse is with you, get medical help at once if you notice any of these.

Danger signs after birth

  • Heavy bleeding, or large clots
  • Fever, chills or feeling very unwell
  • Severe headache or blurred vision
  • Fits (convulsions) or fainting
  • Difficulty breathing or chest pain
  • A wound that is red, hot, swollen or oozing
  • Painful, red, swollen leg
  • Thoughts of harming yourself or your baby

This list is a guide, not medical advice. If you are worried, trust your instinct and go to your nearest hospital.
