A day with your nurse

A gentle rhythm, your way

An example day for a six-month-old. Every baby is different; we follow your routine and your doctor’s advice.

  1. 7:30

    Handover

    You share the night’s news and the plan for the day, then head out with peace of mind.

  2. 8:00

    Feed & play

    Milk feed, nappy, then floor play and tummy time on a clean mat.

  3. 9:30

    Morning nap

    Settled on their back in a safe cot; bottles washed and sterilised.

  4. 11:30

    Lunch & fresh air

    Feeds as you’ve agreed with your health worker, then a short walk in the shade.

  5. 2:00

    Afternoon nap & bath

    Rest, then a warm bath and fresh clothes.

  6. 5:30

    Report & reunion

    Feeds, naps, nappies and a “today they…” moment, written down for you.

Growing up

Little moments we look out for

Our nurses encourage play, talk and movement every day, and share what they notice with you. Babies develop at their own pace; your health worker is the best guide.

Around 3 months

Smiles, coos, lifting the head during tummy time.

Around 6 months

Rolling, sitting with support, laughing, reaching for toys.

Around 9 months

Sitting alone, crawling, babbling “ma-ma”, “da-da”.

Around 12 months

Pulling to stand, first steps along furniture, waving bye-bye.

Remember your child health card: we will remind you of immunisation dates if you’d like.
