Our story

Cooked with integrity, served with love

Eunie's Kitchen Ltd is a team of motivated professionals who want to see Ugandan cuisine, tasty, healthy and organic, appreciated at home and around the world. Led by managing partner Dr. Eunice Adubango, we cook three kinds of food: food for the body, food for the mind through inspirational books, and food for the soul.

“To give our customers a place to celebrate life's special moments by offering the most affordable tasty meals, friendliest service and highest standards of hygiene.”Our mission
