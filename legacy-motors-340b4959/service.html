Why a Subaru specialist?

Subarus are different

Three things that make a Subaru special, and why they deserve a workshop that knows them.

The boxer engine

Horizontally opposed cylinders sit low and flat. Great for balance, but they need the right oil, gaskets and know-how.

Symmetrical AWD

All four wheels driven all the time. Mismatched tyres or neglected fluids strain the drivetrain.

Lineartronic CVT

Smooth and efficient when its fluid is right; costly when ignored. We service it properly.

Book a check if you notice

Warning signs not to ignore

Catching these early is almost always cheaper.

Don’t drive on with…

  • A flashing check-engine light
  • Overheating or a sweet coolant smell
  • Grinding brakes
  • Jerking or slipping when accelerating

Book a check soon for…

  • A steady warning light
  • Knocks over bumps
  • Oil spots where you park
  • Uneven tyre wear
