Genuine matters

Why we insist on genuine parts

Fake and poor-quality parts are common on the market. They look the same, cost a little less, and fail sooner, often taking other parts with them.

Genuine parts

  • Fit exactly as engineered
  • Last their full service life
  • Protect the engine, CVT and AWD
  • Keep your Subaru’s value

Cheap copies

  • Wear out early
  • Can damage other components
  • Mean paying twice
  • Void the “right first time” promise

Parts questions

All parts questions
Where do I find my chassis number?

On your vehicle logbook, and on a plate in the engine bay or at the bottom of the windscreen. A photo on WhatsApp is fine.

Can I buy a part without a service?

Yes. Walk in or order on WhatsApp. We can also fit it for you.

What if the part isn’t in stock?

We tell you the lead time and price to order it in, so you can decide.

Do parts come with a warranty?

Ask us about the warranty on the specific part when you order.
