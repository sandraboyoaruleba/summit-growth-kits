Our story

Bridging the gap between buying and caring

Legacy Motors was founded in 2017 by a founder with over a decade of experience in the service industry.

The idea was simple: too many Subaru owners in Uganda bought a great car and then struggled to keep it that way. Legacy Motors set out to bridge the gap between purchasing a Subaru and maintaining it with care and expertise.

Our name reflects our vision: to build a company that stands the test of time, passing down its commitment to quality, excellence and integrity. Today our focus is genuine Subaru spare parts, oils and lubricants, handled by skilled, qualified technicians, so every vehicle stays reliable for years.
