Know your Subaru

Small habits that save big repairs.

Practical advice from our workshop floor for every Subaru owner in Uganda.

Servicing

Keep your Subaru in top shape with regular servicing

Fresh oil and filters protect the boxer engine. Stick to your interval, and shorten it on dusty roads and in heavy traffic.

Transmission

Take care of your transmission for smooth driving

Jerks, slipping or a whine are early signs. CVT fluid that is checked and changed on time costs far less than a rebuild.

Safety

Don't compromise on safety: check your brake pads

Squealing, grinding or a longer stopping distance mean it's time. Pads are cheap, and discs and accidents are not.

Suspension

A smooth, safe and stable ride starts underneath

Potholes punish shocks and bushes. Knocks over bumps or uneven tyre wear call for a suspension check.
