All questionsAsk on WhatsApp
Do you only work on Subarus?

Yes. Focusing on one brand means our technicians, tools and parts stock are all built around Subaru, so diagnosis is faster and repairs are done right the first time.

Are your spare parts genuine?

We specialise in genuine Subaru spare parts, oils and lubricants. Tell us your model and chassis number and we confirm the correct part before you buy.

Can I buy a part without booking a service?

Of course. Walk in to our Station Road branch or order on WhatsApp, and we can also fit it for you.

How long does a regular service take?

Most routine services are completed the same day. Book ahead and we give you a time estimate.
