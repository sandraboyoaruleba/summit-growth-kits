Primary at Capstone

Every learner known and stretched

In primary, our pupils build the core skills of English, Mathematics, Science and Social Studies, with plenty of reading, spelling and practice. Teachers follow each child’s progress closely and keep parents informed.

Learning goes beyond the classroom: music, sports, clubs and celebrations help pupils grow in confidence and character, true to our motto, Fostering High Achievers.

Questions

About primary

More answers on our FAQ page, or ask us directly.

Ask on WhatsApp
Which classes do you offer?

Primary classes from Primary One upwards. Ask us which classes have space this term.

Do pupils get homework?

Yes, short and regular, so that parents can see what their child is learning and help at home.

How do you report progress?

Every term pupils receive a report card, and parents are invited to meet the class teacher.

Do you offer sports and music?

Yes. Pupils take part in Sports Day, music shows and clubs. See school life.
