A day at Capstone

What a happy day looks like

A typical nursery day. Times are a guide.

  1. 7:30

    Warm welcome

    Greetings at the gate, free play and morning circle.

  2. 8:30

    Learning time

    Phonics, numbers and hands-on activities in small groups.

  3. 10:30

    Break & outdoor play

    A snack, running, games and fresh air.

  4. 12:30

    Lunch & rest

    A healthy meal and quiet rest for the little ones.

  5. 2:00

    Creative afternoon

    Art, music, stories and movement.

  6. 4:00

    Home time

    Pick-up, with daycare continuing for families who need it.

Questions

Parents often ask

The questions we hear most from new families. Can’t find yours?

All questionsAsk on WhatsApp
From what age do you take children?

We welcome children from 1 year old in daycare, then nursery (Baby, Middle and Top class) and primary as they grow.

Where exactly is the school?

We are in Wampewo–Kasangati along Gayaza Road, Wakiso. Call or WhatsApp us and we will send a map pin and directions from the main road.

How much are the fees?

As a guide, fees start from UGX 300,000 a term for daycare (≈ US$81), UGX 350,000 for nursery (≈ US$95) and UGX 400,000 for primary (≈ US$110). These are sample figures for this preview. See admissions & fees.

Can I visit before enrolling?

Absolutely. Book a visit or message us on WhatsApp and we will show you around.
