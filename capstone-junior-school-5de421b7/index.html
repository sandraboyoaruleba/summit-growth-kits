Welcome to Capstone

One caring school, from first steps to Primary Seven

At Capstone Junior School we believe every child is a high achiever in the making. We bring daycare, nursery and primary education together under one roof, so your child grows with familiar faces, steady routines and teachers who truly know them.

Our classrooms are bright and busy — full of stories, songs, numbers and questions. Beyond the classroom, children shine at sports day, music shows, end-of-term celebrations and our much-loved annual graduation.

  • Safe, loving daycare for little ones from 1 year
  • Play-based nursery that builds confident readers
  • Strong primary foundation in literacy, numeracy & character
  • Conveniently located in Wampewo–Kasangati, Gayaza Road

What a happy day looks like

A typical nursery day — times are a guide.

  1. 7:30

    Warm welcome

    Greetings at the gate, free play and morning circle.

  2. 8:30

    Learning time

    Art, music, stories and movement.

  6. 4:00

    Home time

Questions

Parents often ask

From what age do you take children?

We welcome children from 1 year old in our daycare, then nursery and primary as they grow.

Where exactly is the school?

We are in Wampewo–Kasangati along Gayaza Road, Wakiso. Call us and we'll send a pin and directions from the main road.

Do you offer transport and meals?

Yes — ask us about school van routes and our daily meals for daycare and nursery children.

Can I visit before enrolling?

Absolutely. Book a visit with the form below or on WhatsApp and we'll show you around.

Is it a day or boarding school?

Capstone is a day school, with daycare for younger children.
