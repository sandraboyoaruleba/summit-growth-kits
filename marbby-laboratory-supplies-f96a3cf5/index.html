01 — Who we supply

Built around four kinds of laboratory

Physics, chemistry and biology practical requirements, from reagents and indicators to apparatus, specimens and lab safety gear. Ready before practical season.

MED

Health facilities

Clinical lab consumables, stains, reagents, glassware and equipment for hospitals, health centres and diagnostic laboratories.

RES

Research

Analytical-grade chemicals, precision glassware and equipment for universities, institutes and project laboratories.

DRY

Dairy industry

Milk quality testing supplies for collection centres, processors and cooperatives: testing reagents, lactometers, glassware and more.

07 — FAQ

Questions, answered

Can you supply a full school practical requirements list?

Yes. Send the list exactly as your teachers wrote it, whether physics, chemistry or biology, and we'll quote line by line.

Do you deliver outside Kampala?

We deliver to schools and facilities across Uganda. Delivery charges depend on location and order size.

Do you issue proforma invoices for procurement?

Yes. Our quotations can be issued as proforma invoices for your bursar, procurement or accounts office.

Can I get safety data sheets for chemicals?

Ask for an SDS with your quotation and we'll share what is available from the manufacturer.

Do you supply small orders?

Yes. From a single box of slides to a complete new lab set-up.
