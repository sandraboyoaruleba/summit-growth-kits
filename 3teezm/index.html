Behind the brand

One woman. Three ways to make your day better.

3TeezM is run by Racheal Mirembe Lukoma, an HR practitioner with a passion for good food. 3TeezM brings together three things she cares about, in one small and personal business: quality chicken and sausages, home-baked cookies, and HR support for organisations.

It sounds like an unusual mix, but it all comes from the same place: looking after people. Whether that's your family at dinner, your guests at a party or your team at work, you get the same care, honesty and attention to detail.

FAQ

Good to know.

Are the sausages genuine Farmer's Choice?

Yes. We sell genuine Farmer's Choice sausages, kept chilled until they reach you.

Do you deliver?

Yes, around Kampala and nearby areas. Small delivery fees may apply depending on distance; pick-up is also available.

How far ahead should I order for an event?

For bulk chicken, sausages or cookie trays, 2–3 days' notice helps us guarantee quantities.

What size of organisation do you do HR for?

We focus on small and medium organisations: start-ups, schools, NGOs, shops and family businesses who need professional HR support without a full-time HR department.

How is HR consulting charged?

Per project (for example, a staff handbook) or on a monthly retainer for ongoing support. We'll quote clearly after a first conversation.
