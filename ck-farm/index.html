Our story

Rooted in the soil, run by family.

CK Farm is led by Cornelia Asiimwe. What started six years ago has grown into eight productive acres of coffee and bananas, worked by a dedicated team of six to ten people.

We believe good produce starts with healthy soil and honest work. Every coffee tree is pruned and tended by hand, and our bananas grow between the rows, shading the young coffee and feeding the soil as their leaves fall.

Our promise: produce we would proudly serve at our own table, grown in a way that keeps the land healthy for the next generation.

Better together

Why we grow coffee under bananas

Shade

Banana leaves protect young coffee from harsh sun and heat.

Soil

Fallen leaves and stems become mulch, keeping moisture in and feeding the ground.

Steady income

Bananas give harvests all year while coffee follows its seasons.

From farm to you

The journey of every harvest

  1. Grow

    Trees are pruned, weeded and mulched by hand through the year.

  2. Pick

    Only ripe red cherries and mature banana bunches are harvested.

  3. Dry & sort

    Coffee is sun-dried on clean surfaces and sorted for quality.

  4. Deliver

    Bagged and delivered to buyers, or collected at the farm.

Questions

Good to know

When is coffee available?

Coffee is harvested in seasons. Our main harvests run in the middle and end of the year. Message us to reserve from the next harvest.

Are bananas available all year?

Yes. Bananas fruit throughout the year, so we can supply regular orders.

Can I visit the farm?

Buyers and partners are welcome to visit by appointment. Send us a WhatsApp message to arrange a day.

Do you deliver?

We can arrange delivery for larger orders. Smaller orders can be collected from the farm.
