Before your photo

Passport photo tips

  • Wear a dark top with a collar; avoid white on a white background.
  • No glasses or caps for most passport and visa photos.
  • Natural look: face straight on, mouth closed, ears visible if required.
  • Tell us the purpose (passport, visa, ID, school) so we use the right size.

How soon do I get my event photos?

Usually within a week for events, and one to two weeks for full ceremonies. Passport photos are ready in minutes.

Do you travel outside town?

Yes. Travel and accommodation outside town are quoted separately.

Can I print photos from my phone?

Yes. Send them on WhatsApp in the best quality you have and choose the sizes.

How much is the deposit?

A deposit secures your date; the balance is paid on the day or on delivery.
