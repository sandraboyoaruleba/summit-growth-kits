Our story

Why print, photo and stationery?

Because that is how customers actually need them: the CV and the passport photo for the job application; the graduation portrait and the frame; the school list and the photocopies.

Brian describes the business simply: printery services, photography services and stationery services. Customers come for the quality and stay because they know him. The challenge he named himself is that when he’s away, the customers wait.

So the Centre now works a little differently. You can send your job ahead on WhatsApp or email at any time, with everything written out, and collect it when it’s ready. Same quality, same friendly service, no queue.
