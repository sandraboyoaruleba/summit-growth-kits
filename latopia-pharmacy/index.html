Health tips

Little habits, healthier you.

Friendly, practical advice from your pharmacy. For anything serious, always see a doctor.

🦟

Test before you treat malaria

Not every fever is malaria. A quick test means the right medicine, and finishing the full dose stops it coming back.

💊

Finish your antibiotics

Stopping early when you feel better can make infections return stronger. Take the full course as directed.

☀️

Sunscreen isn't only for holidays

Daily SPF helps prevent dark spots and uneven tone. Ask us for one that suits your skin.

❤️

Check your BP every few months

High blood pressure is silent. A two-minute check at the pharmacy can protect your heart.
