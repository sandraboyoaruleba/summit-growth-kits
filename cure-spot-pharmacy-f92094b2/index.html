From the counter

Simple health tips

Medicines

Finish the full course

Even when you feel better, complete antibiotics exactly as prescribed. Stopping early can let infections come back stronger.

Malaria

Test before you treat

Fever doesn’t always mean malaria. Get tested first, sleep under a treated net, and seek care quickly for children.

Skin

Sunscreen is for everyone

Kampala’s sun is strong all year. A daily SPF helps prevent dark spots and uneven tone. Ask us which suits your skin.

Home

Check your first-aid kit

Every few months, replace expired items and restock plasters, antiseptic, gauze, a thermometer and pain relief.

General information only, not a diagnosis. Always follow your doctor’s or pharmacist’s advice for your situation, and seek care urgently for severe symptoms.

All questionsAsk on WhatsApp
Can I order medicines on WhatsApp?

Yes. Send your list or a clear photo of your prescription to +256 785 139 523. We confirm stock and price before anything is prepared.

Do you deliver?

Yes, around Bukoto, Kisaasi, Ntinda and Kyanja, from UGX 3,000. Ask us about your area.

Do I need a prescription?

Prescription-only medicines need a valid prescription from a qualified health worker. Over-the-counter products, supplies and cosmetics do not.

How can I pay?

Cash or Mobile Money (MTN & Airtel).
