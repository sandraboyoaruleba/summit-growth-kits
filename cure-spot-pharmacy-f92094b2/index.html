About Cure Spot

Care that feels personal, right on your doorstep.

Cure Spot Pharmacy is a community pharmacy on Bukoto–Kisaasi Road in Kampala. We stock the medicines, medical supplies and cosmetics your family reaches for every day — and we make it easy to get them, whether you pop into the shop or send us a message on WhatsApp.

Our small, dedicated team takes time to listen, explains how to take your medicines safely, and helps you find the right product for your skin, your baby or your first-aid kit.

From the counter

Simple health tips

Medicines

Finish the full course

Even when you feel better, complete antibiotics exactly as prescribed. Stopping early can let infections return stronger.

Malaria

Test before you treat

Fever doesn't always mean malaria. Get tested first, sleep under a treated net, and seek care quickly for children.

Skin

Sunscreen is for everyone

Kampala's sun is strong all year. A daily SPF helps prevent dark spots and uneven tone — ask us which suits your skin.

Home

Check your first-aid kit

Every few months, replace expired items and restock plasters, antiseptic, gauze, a thermometer and pain relief.

General information only — always follow your doctor's or pharmacist's advice for your situation.

Frequently asked questions

Still unsure? Send us a WhatsApp message — we're happy to help.

Can I order medicines on WhatsApp?

Yes. Send your list or a clear photo of your prescription to +256 785 139 523. We'll confirm stock and price before anything is prepared.

Do you deliver?

We arrange deliveries for WhatsApp orders. Delivery is available around Bukoto, Kisaasi, Ntinda and Kyanja — ask us about your area and the delivery fee.

Do I need a prescription?

Prescription-only medicines require a valid prescription from a qualified health worker. Over-the-counter products, supplies and cosmetics do not.

How can I pay?

We accept cash and Mobile Money (MTN & Airtel).

What are your opening hours?

Monday to Sunday, 8:00am – 10:00pm.
