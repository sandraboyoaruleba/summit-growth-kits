Care that feels personal, right on your doorstep

Our story

A pharmacy for the neighbourhood

Cure Spot began with a simple idea: the people of Bukoto and Kisaasi deserve a pharmacy where they are known by name, where questions are welcome and where genuine products are the only kind on the shelf.

From the start, Cure Spot has served customers two ways: at the counter on Bukoto–Kisaasi Road, and on WhatsApp for families, workers and older customers who can’t always come in. Send a list or a photo of a prescription, and the team confirms stock and price before anything is prepared.

Today a team of 6–10 people keeps the shop running every day, and more of our neighbours find us through friends, Instagram and WhatsApp each month.
