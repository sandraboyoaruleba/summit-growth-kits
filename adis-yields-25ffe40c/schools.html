Simple, filling, affordable

Through the term

What a term looks like

  1. Before term

    Agree the plan

    Learner numbers, egg days, delivery day and price, confirmed on WhatsApp and in writing.

  2. Week 1

    First delivery

    Crates delivered to the school store with a delivery note for the matron or storekeeper.

  3. Every week

    Same day, same crates

    Deliveries arrive before your egg days. Numbers can change with a day’s notice.

  4. Mid-term

    Quick check-in

    We ask the kitchen how it’s going and adjust for visiting days or exams.

  5. End of term

    One clear invoice

    All deliveries listed for the bursar. We plan next term’s supply before you close.

School questions

For bursars and cooks

Can the price be fixed for the whole term?

Yes, we can agree one price per crate for the term when you commit to a weekly quantity.

How do we pay?

By Mobile Money or bank transfer, per delivery, monthly or at the end of term by agreement.

Can you deliver outside Kampala?

We deliver across Kampala and Wakiso and to nearby towns. For schools further away, ask us: larger weekly orders may make it possible.

What if our numbers change mid-term?

Send a WhatsApp message a day before your delivery day and we adjust the crates.

Plan next term’s eggs today

Tell us your number of learners and egg days. We reply with a weekly plan and a price for the term.
