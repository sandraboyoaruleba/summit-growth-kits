All questionsAsk on WhatsApp
How many eggs are in a crate?

A standard crate (tray) holds 30 eggs. Wholesale and school orders are counted in crates.

Do you deliver?

Yes. We deliver to homes, shops, supermarkets and schools. Bulk orders within Kampala, Wakiso and nearby towns are delivered free; small orders may carry a small delivery fee.

Can I set up a regular order?

Absolutely. Many buyers take a fixed number of crates every week, day or school term. We reserve your eggs from each day’s collection, so you are never left without stock.

How fresh are the eggs?

We collect eggs from our hens every day and deliver as soon as possible, typically within a few days of laying.
