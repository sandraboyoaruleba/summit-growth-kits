Our farm

From our hen house to your counter, the same day.

Adis Yields started with a simple belief: Ugandan families and businesses deserve eggs they can rely on. Today our flock of 1,400 layers gives us a steady daily harvest, which we collect, sort and crate by hand.

Because we control every step from feeding to delivery, we can promise freshness that market-hopped eggs simply can't match. Your eggs come straight from us.

  • Cracked or dirty eggs removed before crating

FAQ

Good questions.

Can't find your answer? Ask us on WhatsApp.

How many eggs are in a crate?

A standard crate (tray) holds 30 eggs.

Do you deliver?

Yes. We deliver to homes, shops, supermarkets and schools. Bulk orders within Kampala and nearby areas are delivered free; small orders may carry a small delivery fee.

Can I set up a regular order?

Absolutely. Many of our customers take a fixed number of crates every week, day or school term. We reserve your eggs from each day's collection.

How fresh are the eggs?

We collect eggs from our hens daily and deliver them as soon as possible, typically within a day or two of laying.

How should I store eggs?

Keep eggs in a cool, dry place away from direct sunlight, pointed end down. Refrigerated eggs keep even longer. Don't wash them until you're ready to use them.
