How many crates?

A quick guide for planning

Not sure how much to order? These rough numbers help.

  • A family of five eating eggs most mornings uses about 1 crate a week.
  • A small shop selling eggs singly often moves 5–10 crates a week.
  • A school of 400 learners serving one egg twice a week needs about 27 crates a week.
  • A busy hotel breakfast of 60 guests uses about 2 crates a day.

Simple arithmetic, 30 eggs to a crate. We’ll help you work out your exact number.
