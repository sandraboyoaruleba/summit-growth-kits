my story

Hello, I’m Esther Catherine.

Kathryn Cakes is my home bakery. Every cake is mixed, baked and decorated by me, in small batches, with good ingredients and a lot of care.

Most of my customers find me online and order straight from their phone. Whether it’s a simple birthday cake for the office or a showstopper for your introduction ceremony, I’ll work with you on the flavour and design until it’s exactly right.

I started baking for family celebrations, and friends soon began asking for cakes of their own. Today I bake for birthdays, weddings, introductions, graduations and every function in between.

— Esther Catherine Nakadama

the name

Why “Kathryn”?

Kathryn is a form of Catherine, Esther’s own middle name. Putting her name on the box is a promise: every cake is one she would be proud to serve at her own table.

Kathryn Cakes is Esther’s bakery in Uganda. It is not connected to similarly named bakeries abroad.
