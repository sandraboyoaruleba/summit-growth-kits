Questions

What customers ask before they order. Can’t find yours?

All questionsAsk Esther
How far in advance should I order?

At least 3 days for most cakes, and 2 weeks or more for wedding and tiered cakes. Last-minute? Message us and we’ll try!

Can you copy a cake design from a photo?

Yes. Send the photo on WhatsApp and we’ll recreate it or give it our own twist, and confirm the price.

Do you deliver?

Yes, we deliver within Kampala and nearby areas for a small fee depending on distance.

How do I pay?

Mobile Money or cash. A deposit confirms your order, with the balance on pick-up or delivery.
