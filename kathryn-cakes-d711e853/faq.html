Ordering

How far in advance should I order?

At least 3 days for most cakes, and 2 weeks or more for wedding and tiered cakes. Last-minute? Message us and we’ll try!

How do I order?

Send a WhatsApp message to +256 774 688 199, or fill in the order form, which opens WhatsApp with your order already typed.

Can you copy a cake design from a photo?

Yes. Send the photo on WhatsApp and we’ll recreate it or give it our own twist, and confirm the price.

Can I change my order?

Messages and colours can usually change up to 2 days before. Size or design changes depend on what has been prepared; we’ll always tell you first.

Cakes & sizes

Which flavours do you bake?

Classic vanilla, rich chocolate, red velvet, marble, black forest, fruit cake and lemon or orange, plus cupcakes. See flavours & prices.

How big a cake do I need?

A 1 kg cake serves about 10–12 people. For 50 guests or more we suggest a tiered cake or extra cutting cake. See the size guide.

Do you make cakes for introductions (Kwanjula / Kuhingira)?

Yes. Rich fruit cake or a tiered design in your family’s colours. See introduction cakes.

Can you bake for allergies?

Please tell us about any allergy before ordering. We’ll be honest about what we can safely make in a home kitchen.

Prices & payment

How much is a cake?

Cakes start from UGX 60,000 (≈ US$16) for a 1 kg vanilla cake. Tiered cakes start from UGX 350,000 (≈ US$95). You get a clear price before you pay anything.

How do I pay?

Mobile Money or cash. A deposit confirms your order, with the balance on pick-up or delivery.

Can I order from abroad for family in Uganda?

Yes. Many customers send a cake to loved ones at home. Order on WhatsApp, pay by Mobile Money, and we deliver to your family.

Pick-up & delivery

Do you deliver?

Yes, we deliver within Kampala and nearby areas for a small fee depending on distance.

Where do I pick up?

We share the pick-up location on WhatsApp when your order is confirmed.

Ready to order?

The order form takes a minute and opens WhatsApp for you.
