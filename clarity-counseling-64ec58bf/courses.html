How you'll learn

Learn, observe, practise, apply

The same approach we use in our therapy rooms.

  1. Learn

    Core concepts through short lectures and real case studies.

  2. Observe

    Live demonstrations of counselling techniques in action.

  3. Practise

    Role-play in small groups with feedback from your facilitator.

  4. Apply

    Supervised practice with real clients, and reflection on what worked.

Course questions

Before you enrol

I have no psychology background. Can I join?

Yes. Our Certificate and short course are designed for beginners. Teachers, HR professionals, NGO workers, ministry leaders, medics and parents all study with us.

Are the Diploma and Certificate recognised?

Clarity is TVETA-registered and NITA-accredited, and the Diploma and Certificate are examined by KNEC. We also guide graduates on the path to registration with the Counsellors & Psychologists Board.

Can I pay in instalments?

Yes. The Certificate can be paid from KES 10,000 a month, and Diploma fees are paid semester by semester, with monthly plans available.

Can I study from outside Nairobi or abroad?

Yes. Live online classes let you join from anywhere in Kenya or the diaspora. Practicum arrangements are discussed with admissions.

How do I pay the Leadership Program deposit?

Pay the KES 15,000 deposit by M-Pesa Paybill 9009090, account BLT, then send your confirmation on WhatsApp.
