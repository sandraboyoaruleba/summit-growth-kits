Starting therapy

I've never had therapy. What happens in the first session?

It's about getting comfortable. Your counsellor gets to know you and what brought you in, explains how confidentiality works, and you share only what you're ready to share. Feeling nervous is completely normal.

How long is a session, and how often will we meet?

Standard sessions are 50 minutes (group sessions are 90). Most people start weekly, then move to every two weeks or monthly. You and your counsellor decide together.

How many sessions will I need?

It's different for everyone. Some people feel ready to stop after a few sessions; others continue for months. Your counsellor checks in on your progress regularly, and you're always free to decide.

Do I need a diagnosis or a referral?

No. You don't need a formal diagnosis or a doctor's letter to start. Many people come simply because life feels heavy.

Can I choose or switch my therapist?

Yes. A good fit matters. You can ask for a specific counsellor, and if your match isn't right after a session, we'll rematch you at no extra cost.

Can I bring my child, or come as a couple?

Yes. Couples attend together (one partner can start alone if the other is hesitant). We see children and teens from age 4 to 18; younger children often have parents join part of the session, and teens usually meet their counsellor one-on-one with parent check-ins.

Is online therapy a good option?

Many clients find online sessions work well, especially Kenyans abroad, busy professionals and people outside Nairobi. You can also mix online and in-person sessions. See online therapy.

Which languages can I use?

English or Kiswahili, and Sheng online, or a mix of whatever feels natural.

Privacy & safety

Is what I share confidential?

Yes. Everything discussed in therapy is confidential under Kenyan counselling ethics. The only exceptions are where someone's life or safety is at serious risk, and your counsellor explains this in your first session.

Is Clarity an emergency service?

No. Our WhatsApp line and booking form are not watched around the clock. If you or someone else is in immediate danger, call 999 or 112, Kenya Red Cross on 1199, or go to the nearest hospital. See urgent help.

What should I write on WhatsApp when I book?

Just your name, the kind of support you want and a good time. Please don't share sensitive details in the message; you can tell your counsellor in session.

If my employer pays, will they know what I said?

No. Through workplace programmes, HR only ever receives anonymised, overall figures, never names or session content.

Fees & insurance

How much does therapy cost?

Individual KES 3,500 (≈ US$27), couples KES 4,500 (≈ US$35), child and teen KES 3,500, family KES 6,000 (≈ US$47) and group sessions from KES 2,500 per person. See all fees.

Do you accept medical insurance?

We work with several Kenyan insurers and corporate schemes. Cover depends on your policy, so send us your insurer's name on WhatsApp and we'll check for you.

How can I pay?

M-Pesa, card, or through our Pesapal page. Clients abroad can also pay by PayPal or Wise.

Do I need to book before visiting?

Yes, for therapy, so we can make sure a room and the right counsellor are free. Walk-ins are welcome for course admissions enquiries.

Courses

What are the entry requirements?

Diploma: KCSE C- or a Certificate in Counselling Psychology. Certificate: KCSE grade D or above. Short courses are open to everyone.

Can I study while working full-time?

Yes. We offer evening classes, Saturday classes and live online learning.

Are your courses recognised?

Clarity is TVETA-registered and NITA-accredited, and our Diploma and Certificate are examined by KNEC.

Still have a question?

Our intake team replies on WhatsApp during office hours, usually within a couple of hours.
