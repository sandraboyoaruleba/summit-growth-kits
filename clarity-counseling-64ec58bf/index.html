Starting is simpler than it feels

Three gentle steps

  1. Choose your therapyIndividual, couples, child, family or group. Not sure? Message us and we'll help you decide.
  2. Pick your formatIn person at our Nairobi office, or by secure video call from anywhere.
  3. Meet your counsellorWe match you with a licensed counsellor suited to your goals. If the fit isn't right, we'll rematch you at no extra cost.

What you share stays private. Everything discussed in therapy is confidential under Kenyan counselling ethics. The only exceptions are where someone's life or safety is at serious risk, and your counsellor explains this in your first session.

Questions

Before your first session

Anything else on your mind? Ask us on WhatsApp.

How long is a session, and how often will we meet?

Standard sessions are 50 minutes (group sessions are 90). Most people start weekly, then move to every two weeks or monthly. You and your counsellor decide together.

How many sessions will I need?

It's different for everyone. Some people feel ready to stop after a few sessions; others continue for months. Your counsellor checks in on your progress regularly, and you're always free to decide.

I've never had therapy. What happens in the first session?

It's about getting comfortable. Your counsellor gets to know you and what brought you in, and you share only what you're ready to share. Feeling nervous is completely normal.

Is online therapy a good option?

Many clients find online sessions work well, especially Kenyans abroad, busy professionals and people outside Nairobi. You can also mix online and in-person sessions.

Can I switch therapists?

Yes. A good fit matters. If your matched counsellor isn't right for you, we'll rematch you at no extra cost.

Can I bring my child, or come as a couple?

Yes. Couples attend together from the first session (one partner can start alone if the other is hesitant). For younger children, parents often join part of the session; teens usually meet their counsellor one-on-one with parent check-ins.

Is Clarity an emergency service?

No. If you or someone else is in immediate danger, please use the urgent help numbers below or go to the nearest hospital emergency department.
