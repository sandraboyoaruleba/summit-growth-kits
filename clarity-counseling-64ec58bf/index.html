You don't have to carry it alone

How do you know it's time to talk to someone?

Life can feel overwhelming, whether it's stress, grief, a big transition, or pain you haven't found words for yet. You don't need a formal diagnosis to benefit from therapy. If any of these sound familiar, a counsellor can help you make sense of it, one session at a time.

Talk to a counsellorSee therapy & fees
  • Anxiety, panic and constant worry
  • Depression, low mood or losing motivation
  • Relationship conflict, breakdown or infidelity
  • Grief, bereavement and major losses
  • Trauma and abuse recovery
  • Workplace stress, burnout and career crisis
  • Parenting struggles and children's behaviour
  • Self-esteem, identity and finding yourself
  • Life transitions: marriage, relocation, retirement
  • Anger and emotional regulation
  • Sex and intimacy concerns
  • Diaspora adjustment and homesickness

Starting is simpler than it feels

Three gentle steps

  1. Choose your therapy

    Individual, couples, child, family or group. Not sure? Message us and we'll help you decide before you book.

  2. Pick your format

    In person at Finance House, Loita Street, or by secure video call from anywhere in Kenya or abroad.

  3. Meet your counsellor

    We match you with a licensed counsellor suited to your goals. If the fit isn't right, we rematch you at no extra cost.

What you share stays private. Everything discussed in therapy is confidential under Kenyan counselling ethics. The only exceptions are where someone's life or safety is at serious risk, and your counsellor explains this in your first session.

If you need help right now

Clarity is not an emergency service

Our booking form and WhatsApp line are not watched around the clock. If you or someone you know is in immediate danger or thinking about ending their life, please reach out now.

What to do in a crisis
  • 999 or 112Police and ambulance emergency
  • 1199Kenya Red Cross, toll-free
  • 0722 178 177Befrienders Kenya: emotional support and suicide prevention, by call or text
  • Nearest hospitalGo to the emergency department, or take the person there

Take the first step, gently

Send a few words on WhatsApp or fill in the short booking form. Our intake team confirms a slot within 24 hours.
