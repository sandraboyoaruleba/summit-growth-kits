About Clarity

Where healing begins, growth thrives, and careers are built

Clarity Counseling & Training Centre is a Nairobi counselling practice and counsellor-training institution. We bring compassionate, evidence-based therapy and nationally recognised counsellor education together under one roof.

  • KCPA accredited
  • TVETA registered
  • Local & diaspora clients

Our story

A sanctuary for healing and professional growth

Maybe you've been searching for someone to talk to, someone who truly gets it. You're emotionally exhausted, navigating stress, relationship struggles, or carrying the silent weight of anxiety that no one else sees.

Or perhaps you've always felt drawn to mental health, wondering if you could turn your compassion into a rewarding career.

Jane Gacheru founded Clarity for both of you. Today our licensed counsellors see individuals, couples, children and families in Nairobi CBD and online, while our training school prepares the next generation of counsellors through KNEC-examined, TVETA-registered courses with supervised practice.

Since we started, we have worked with more than 40 organisations across Kenya, from the Judiciary to NGOs and schools, and our graduates now work in hospitals, HR teams, NGOs and private practice.

The Clarity promise

The meaning behind the name

Why “Clarity”?

Most people who come to us aren't looking for a diagnosis. They want to see their situation clearly again, and to know their next step. Our logo's three figures stand for the people we serve: the person who is hurting, the people who love them, and the counsellor walking alongside.

Genuine compassion

A safe, non-judgmental space where you share only what you're ready to share.

Clinical integrity

Evidence-based care from licensed counsellors who follow KCPA's ethical code.

Recognised standards

TVETA-registered, NITA-accredited training, with KNEC-examined Diploma and Certificate courses.

Local & global

A central Nairobi office plus secure online sessions for Kenyans everywhere.

Accreditation

Trusted, recognised standards

So that every session and every certificate carries real weight.

KCPA accredited

Kenya Counselling & Psychological Association, No. KCPA/INST/0147/019.

NITA accredited

National Industrial Training Authority training centre, No. NITA/TRN/2202. Eligible employer training can be claimed through the NITA levy.

TVETA registered

Technical & Vocational Education and Training Authority, No. TVETA/PRIVATE/TVC/0123/2026. Diploma and Certificate examined by KNEC.

Our counsellors are recognised by the Counsellors & Psychologists Board (CPB).
