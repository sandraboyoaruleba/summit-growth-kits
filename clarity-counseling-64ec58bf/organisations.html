What we offer

Support for every part of your organisation

Confidential counselling

Private in-person or video sessions for staff dealing with stress, anxiety, depression, burnout, grief or relationship pressure.

Critical incident debriefing

Group and individual support after a sudden death, an accident, a robbery or another traumatic event at work.

Manager mental health training

Helping supervisors spot early distress, offer psychological first aid and support struggling team members.

Wellness workshops

Interactive sessions on stress, burnout, work-life balance, emotional intelligence and healthy communication.

NGO & humanitarian teams

Support for compassion fatigue and vicarious trauma, pre-deployment and post-mission debriefs, and wellness days for field teams.

Leadership coaching

Coaching for leaders on emotional intelligence and decision-making under pressure, plus our Business Leader's Transformation Program.

Confidential & stigma-free

Your people reach us directly

Privacy worries and workplace stigma stop many people from asking for help. So staff contact Clarity directly, with no HR approval needed.

  • Direct booking by phone, WhatsApp or our booking form
  • Session notes stay private. HR receives anonymised usage and trend reports only
  • Flexible delivery: at our Nairobi office, on-site at your premises, or by secure video
  • NITA levy: eligible training can be claimed back through the National Industrial Training Authority
  • Nationwide: staff outside Nairobi join by video or phone

How we roll out

Four steps, fully measurable

  1. Needs assessment

    We look at your headcount, locations, risks and HR priorities, then design your plan.

  2. Launch & anti-stigma

    A kickoff session and simple guides so staff know how to reach us privately.

  3. Confidential care

    Staff book sessions directly by phone, WhatsApp or at our Nairobi office.

  4. Quarterly insights

    Anonymised reports on usage and stress trends, with practical recommendations.
