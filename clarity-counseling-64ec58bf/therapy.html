What to expect

Your first session

It's normal to feel nervous. The first session is mostly about getting comfortable and understanding what brought you in.

  1. Before: a short message

    Book online or on WhatsApp. Our intake team confirms your time, your counsellor and the fee within 24 hours, and sends a video link if you're online.

  2. During: you set the pace

    Your counsellor explains confidentiality and its limits, asks about what's on your mind, and you share only what you're ready to share.

  3. After: a plan that suits you

    Together you agree how often to meet (often weekly at first) and what you'd like to change. You're always free to pause or stop.

Confidential, with clear limits. What you share stays private under Kenyan counselling ethics. The only exceptions are a serious risk to your life or someone else's, which your counsellor explains in your first session.
