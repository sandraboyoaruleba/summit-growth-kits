Online therapy questions

Good to know

Are online sessions private?

Yes. Sessions run on Zoom or Google Meet with a private link, and your counsellor follows the same confidentiality rules as in person. Choose a quiet, private spot on your side, and use headphones if others are at home.

Is online therapy a good option for me?

Many clients find online sessions work well, especially Kenyans abroad, busy professionals and people outside Nairobi. If video isn't right for what you're going through, your counsellor will say so and suggest options.

How do I pay from outside Kenya?

By international card (Visa, Mastercard), M-Pesa, PayPal or Wise. You can also pay through Clarity's Pesapal page.

What if I'm in crisis abroad?

Online therapy is not an emergency service. If you're in danger, call your local emergency number (for example 999 in the UK, 911 in the US and Canada, or 112 across the EU) or go to the nearest hospital. In Kenya, see our urgent help page.
