Videography

Storytelling that inspires, informs and engages

Video is where JorartS MEDIA started. We have filmed documentaries on Uganda’s landscapes and geology, health films and openers, promos for farming, faith and events, and our own trips, from Jinja to Ntoroko and the Rwenzori region.

Every film starts with your goal and your audience. We plan the shots, capture clean sound, then shape the story in the edit with colour, music and motion until it feels right.

What to expect

How a project runs

  1. 01Brief

    You tell us the goal, audience, deadline and budget. We ask the right questions.

  2. 02Concept

    Moodboards, scripts, shot lists or design directions, agreed before we start.

  3. 03Create

    The shoot, the design work, the interviews: produced with care and the right gear.

  4. 04Edit

    Colour, sound, motion and revisions until it feels right.

  5. 05Deliver

    Files sized for every platform, on time and ready to publish.

Questions

Good to know

More answers on the FAQ page.

Ask on WhatsApp
Do you write the script?

Yes. We can write it from your brief, or polish a script you already have.

Can you film in more than one place?

Yes. We travel across Uganda. Transport and accommodation outside Kampala are quoted separately.

Who owns the final video?

You do. Once the final invoice is paid, you can use the film anywhere. We may show it in our portfolio unless you ask us not to.

Do you offer drone shots?

Ask us. Aerial shots depend on the location, permits and weather.
