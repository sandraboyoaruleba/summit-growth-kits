Working with Emet

Who do you work with?

Individuals, families and small business owners in Uganda, and Ugandans living abroad who manage money or projects at home.

Do you only work with businesses?

No. Personal and business finances are often linked, and we help with both.

I’m embarrassed about my finances. Should I still come?

Yes. Most people who contact us feel the same. There is no judgement here, only a clear picture and a plan.

Can you work with me remotely?

Yes. Much of the work happens over WhatsApp, phone and video calls, with in-person meetings in Kampala when helpful.

Consultations & fees

How does the first consultation work?

A free 30-minute conversation, in person or online. You leave with clear next steps either way.

How much do your services cost?

Personal services start from UGX 150,000 (≈ US$40) for a financial health check; family finance plans are UGX 450,000 (≈ US$120); monthly bookkeeping starts from UGX 350,000 (≈ US$95). Sample prices for this preview. See packages & fees.

How do I pay?

By Mobile Money or bank transfer. One-off plans are paid half at the start and half on delivery; monthly services at the start of each month.

Do you sell loans, insurance or investments?

No. Emet gives advice and builds systems. We don’t sell financial products, so our advice is independent.

Confidentiality & results

Is my information kept confidential?

Always. Your financial information is handled with strict confidentiality and is never shared without your consent.

What will you need from me?

Usually recent bank and Mobile Money statements, receipts or sales records for businesses, and a list of what you owe. See what to bring.

Do you do tax returns and payroll?

Ask us. We will tell you honestly what we can do, and point you to the right professional if needed.

How soon will I see a difference?

Most clients feel calmer after the first two weeks, once they can see where money goes. Real change, like savings and debt falling, usually shows within three months of following the plan.
