Services

Practical finance, tailor-made.

No two households or businesses are the same, so no two systems should be. Every engagement starts with listening, then we build what fits.

  1. Bookkeeping & accounts

    Accurate, up-to-date records and clear monthly reports, so you always know where your business stands.

  2. Financial systems & structures

    Simple, custom systems for invoicing, expenses, petty cash and approvals that keep money organised and accountable.

  3. Budgeting & cash-flow planning

    Budgets and cash-flow forecasts for homes and businesses, built around real income and real priorities.

  4. Personal & family finance coaching

    One-on-one guidance on spending, saving, debt and goals, with regular check-ins to keep you on track.

  5. Wealth-building plans

    Turn surplus into assets with an intentional plan for emergency funds, savings and long-term growth.

  6. Financial health check

    A focused review of where you are today, what's leaking and the three changes that will matter most.

FAQ

Questions, answered honestly.

Do you only work with businesses?

No. We work with individuals and families as well as small business owners. Personal and business finances are often linked, and we help with both.

How does the first consultation work?

We start with a 30-minute conversation, in person or online, to understand your situation and goals. You'll leave with clear next steps either way.

Is my information kept confidential?

Always. Your financial information is handled with strict confidentiality and is never shared without your consent.

Can you work with me remotely?

Yes. Much of the work can be done over WhatsApp, phone and video calls, with in-person meetings when helpful.

How much do your services cost?

Fees depend on the scope of work. After the first conversation you'll receive a clear proposal with no surprises.
