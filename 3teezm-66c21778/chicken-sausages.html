About the sausages

Why Farmer’s Choice?

Farmer’s Choice is a long-established Kenyan maker of sausages and pork products, sold across East Africa, including Uganda. Families know the taste, and caterers know they cook evenly and hold their shape.

  • Genuine product, in the maker’s sealed packs.
  • Kept chilled from purchase to your door.
  • Checked dates on every pack before it leaves.

Farmer’s Choice is a trademark of its owner. 3TeezM is an independent seller and not affiliated with the manufacturer.

Questions

Chicken & sausage questions

All questions
Is the chicken live or dressed?

Chicken is supplied cleaned and dressed, ready to cook. Ask if you need a live bird or a particular breed (broiler or local).

How is the meat kept fresh?

Chicken and sausages are kept chilled until handover and delivered in cool bags. Please refrigerate as soon as you receive your order.

Can I order for a restaurant every week?

Yes. Agree a weekly quantity and delivery day, and Racheal reserves your order. Volume rates apply.

What is the minimum order for delivery?

There is no strict minimum, but small orders may carry a delivery fee depending on distance.
