What to expect

Practical, fair and done properly

  • Plain language. No jargon; documents your managers can actually use.
  • Grounded in Ugandan law. Contracts and policies aligned with the Employment Act and current regulations.
  • Confidential. Staff matters and salary details stay private.
  • Built for your size. Simple systems for a team of 5, sturdier ones for 50.
  • You own the work. Every template and document is yours to keep.

HR questions

All HR questions
How is HR consulting charged?

Per project (for example, a staff handbook) or as a monthly retainer for ongoing support. You get a clear quote in UGX after the first conversation.

Do you work outside Kampala?

Yes. Much of the work can be done online, and on-site visits elsewhere in Uganda can be arranged.

Can you help with a difficult staff situation right now?

Yes. Message on WhatsApp with a short, confidential summary and Racheal will advise on the next step.

Is my information kept confidential?

Always. Staff records, salaries and disputes are never shared.
