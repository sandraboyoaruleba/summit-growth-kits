All questionsAsk on WhatsApp
Are the sausages genuine Farmer’s Choice?

Yes. 3TeezM sells genuine Farmer’s Choice sausages, kept chilled until they reach you. 3TeezM is an independent seller, not the manufacturer.

Do you deliver?

Yes, around Kampala and nearby areas, or you can pick up. A small delivery fee may apply depending on distance; Racheal confirms it with your order.

How far ahead should I order for an event?

For bulk chicken, party packs or cookie trays, 2–3 days’ notice helps guarantee quantities. For big events, a week is ideal.

What kind of organisations do you do HR for?

Small and medium organisations: start-ups, schools, NGOs, shops, clinics and family businesses that need professional HR support without a full-time HR department.
