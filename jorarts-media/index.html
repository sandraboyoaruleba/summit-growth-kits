(01) The studio

JorartS MEDIA is a creative media agency specialising in storytelling that inspires, informs and engages. We deliver video production, photography, branding and digital marketing tailored to each client's vision.

Founded and led by Jorvan Mubezi, our small, hands-on team blends creativity with technical know-how — from the first sketch to the final export. Corporate campaigns, school events, social media stories: we help brands connect with their audiences and amplify their presence across platforms.

We've filmed across Uganda — from shoots in Jinja to stories from the Rwenzori region and Ntoroko — and produced logo openers, promos and campaign films on health, faith, farming and Uganda's natural wonders.
