06 Land buyer's FAQ

Good questions to ask before you buy or build.

Not sure what you need? Send us a WhatsApp message and we'll advise.

Why should I use a registered surveyor?

Only registered surveyors can legally carry out cadastral surveys used for titles. Using unqualified "surveyors" is a common cause of land disputes and rejected documents. You can ask any surveyor for their registration details.

What should I check before buying land?

Confirm the title at the land registry, open the boundaries on the ground, check for occupants or claims, and get an independent valuation. We can handle the survey, valuation and site due diligence for you.

How long does a valuation take?

Most standard residential valuations are delivered within 3–7 working days after inspection, depending on location and documents.

How much does a survey or valuation cost?

Fees depend on land size, location and the purpose of the work. We give a written quotation after understanding your needs — just send us the details.

Which areas do you cover?

We are based in Kampala and work across Uganda.
