01 Our story

Accurate boundaries. Honest values.

Land is often a family’s most valuable asset, and one of the most disputed. DLVS was built to take that responsibility seriously.

We are a consultancy in land surveying and property valuation. Our team combines fieldwork experience with sound valuation practice: measuring land accurately, valuing property honestly, communicating clearly and keeping records clients can rely on.

Our clients are landowners and families, banks and lenders, developers and contractors, buyers in the diaspora, and projects and institutions.
