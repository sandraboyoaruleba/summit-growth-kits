How we work, and what it costs

A clear five-step process, a written quote for every job and a fee guide so you can plan ahead.

  • Written quotes
  • Prices in UGX

01 The process

From first call to final report

  1. 01

    Enquiry

    Tell us the land location, size and what you need. We advise on the right service.

  2. 02

    Documents & quote

    We review the title, sale agreement or plans and send a written quotation and timeline.

  3. 03

    Fieldwork

    Our team visits the site for measurements, inspection and evidence. Neighbours and the LC are informed where needed.

  4. 04

    Analysis

    Computations, plans, market evidence and checks, done carefully in-house.

  5. 05

    Delivery

    You receive your plans or signed report, and we walk you through what it means.

03 What changes the fee

Why no two quotes are the same

  • Size and shape. More corners and more acres mean more fieldwork.
  • Location and access. Travel, terrain and bush clearing.
  • Purpose. A bank valuation needs a different report from a family estimate.
  • Documents. Missing plans or disputed marks take extra research.
  • Deadline. Urgent jobs can be arranged where the diary allows.

Good to know

  • A deposit is paid when you accept the quote; the balance on delivery.
  • Bank transfer or Mobile Money.
  • Office hours Mon–Fri 8:00–17:00, Sat 9:00–13:00.
  • Every quote is in writing, in UGX.
