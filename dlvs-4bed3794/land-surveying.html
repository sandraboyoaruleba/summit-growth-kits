02 Boundary opening, step by step

What happens on the day

  1. 01
    We study the documents. Title, deed plan or sale agreement, so we know which marks to look for.
  2. 02
    Neighbours and the LC are informed. Boundary work goes better when everyone knows the date.
  3. 03
    We find and check the marks. Using the plan coordinates, we locate existing marks and re-establish missing ones.
  4. 04
    We show you on the ground. You walk the corners with us and see every mark.
  5. 05
    You get a sketch plan and photos. A clear record to keep with your title.

03 Subdivision

Splitting land for family, sale or development

Subdividing a family plot or a larger estate means survey plans for every new plot and a mutation through the land office.

  • We plan the new plots with you: access roads, sizes and shapes that make sense.
  • We survey, mark and prepare the plans for each new plot.
  • We prepare the survey documents for the mutation and explain the land-office steps that follow.

Land office timelines are outside our control; we tell you honestly what to expect.
