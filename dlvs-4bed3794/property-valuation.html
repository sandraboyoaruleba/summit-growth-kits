02 Inside a DLVS report

What your report contains

  • Purpose and basis of value, stated clearly at the start.
  • Property details: location, tenure, title particulars, land size and buildings.
  • Inspection notes and photos of the land and every building.
  • Market evidence: comparable sales and rents, and how they were adjusted.
  • The values: market value, and forced sale value where a lender needs it.
  • Assumptions and limits, so nobody is surprised later.

04 Market value vs forced sale value

Two numbers, two jobs

Market value is the price a willing buyer and a willing seller would agree on after proper marketing.

Forced sale value is what the property might fetch if it had to be sold quickly. Banks use it to judge the security behind a loan.
