Transport

How much does school transport cost?

Fees depend on distance and are paid per term: from UGX 350,000 per term for both ways (≈ US$95) and from UGX 220,000 for one way (≈ US$60). These are sample fees. Send us your area and school for your exact fee.

Which schools and areas do you cover?

We run routes in and around Kampala. Send us your home area and school on WhatsApp and we will confirm.

Is there an adult with the children besides the driver?

Yes. A child minder rides with the children, helps with seat belts and hands each child over at school and at home.

How will I know my child has arrived?

We send a WhatsApp message when children arrive at school and when they are dropped home.

What if we are late for pick-up?

The van waits a few minutes, then continues so other children are not late. Message us as early as you can and we will agree a plan.

Who can collect my child in the afternoon?

Only the adults you name when you register. Tell us in advance if someone new will collect, with their name and phone number.

Christian training

What ages is the Christian training for?

Children aged about 4 to 13, in three age groups.

Is it for children from any church?

Yes. Children from every church background are welcome, and so are children whose families are exploring faith. We teach from the Bible in a way children understand.

Do children need to use the transport to join the training?

No. Families can choose transport, Christian training or both.

What should my child bring?

Comfortable clothes, a water bottle and a smile. We provide a snack, Bibles and craft materials.

Fees & registration

How do I pay?

Per term, by Mobile Money or bank, before the first week. We send a receipt on WhatsApp.

Are there sibling discounts?

Yes, ask us when you register more than one child.

Can our school partner with you?

Yes. We welcome schools looking for dependable transport or a Christian enrichment programme. See For schools.

Still wondering about something?

Message Norah’s team on WhatsApp. We usually reply the same day.
