Our story

Every child, safe and growing

Christian Kids Hub brings two things parents care about under one roof: getting children to school safely, and helping them grow in faith and character.

How it started

Two needs, one trusted team

Every school morning in Kampala, parents face the same question: who can I trust to get my child to school safely and on time? And on weekends and holidays, a second one: how do I help my child grow in faith, good character and confidence?

Norah Namuyomba started Christian Kids Hub to answer both. What began with a few families who needed a dependable school run has grown into a team of six to ten people who drive, mind, teach and pray for the children in their care.

We keep it simple: show up on time, treat every child like our own, keep parents informed, and point children to Jesus in everything we do.

What we believe

Our values

  • Safety first

    Seat belts, careful drivers, a minder on board and handover to a known adult. Every day.

  • Faithful in small things

    On time, every time. Reliability is how we show love to busy parents.

  • Rooted in the Word

    Bible-based teaching that children understand, remember and live out.

  • Partners with parents

    You are your child’s first teacher. We keep you informed and work with you.

The team
