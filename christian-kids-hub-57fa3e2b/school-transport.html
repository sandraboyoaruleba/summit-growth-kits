A day with us

From your gate to the school gate

Here is what a typical school day looks like. Exact times depend on your route.

  1. 6:15am

    Morning pick-up

    We collect your child from an agreed pick-up point near home. Please be ready five minutes early.

  2. On the road

    A calm, cheerful ride

    Seat belts on, a short prayer and songs on the way. No rushing, no overcrowding.

  3. 7:30am

    Safe drop-off

    Children are handed over at school, not just left at the gate. Parents get a WhatsApp “arrived”.

  4. 5:00pm

    Home again

    Afternoon pick-up from school and drop-off back home to a waiting adult.

Our safety promise

Seven things we do on every trip

  • Experienced, carefully selected drivers
  • A child minder on board
  • One seat, one child, seat belts on
  • Regular vehicle checks and servicing
  • Children handed only to a known, named adult
  • WhatsApp updates when children arrive
  • No phones in the driver’s hand while driving

Transport questions

Good to know

All questions
What happens if we are late for pick-up?

The van waits a few minutes, then continues so other children are not late. Message us early if you are running behind and we will agree a plan.

Who can collect my child in the afternoon?

Only the adults you name on registration. If someone new will collect, tell us on WhatsApp in advance with their name and phone number.

Can you collect from a school you don’t already serve?

Often, yes. Send us the school and your home area and we will check whether it fits a route.

Ready to get your child on board?

Tell us your child’s school and home area, and we’ll come back to you with the route, the times and the fee.
