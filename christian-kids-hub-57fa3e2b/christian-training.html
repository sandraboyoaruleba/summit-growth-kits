A Saturday morning

What a session looks like

A sample plan. Each term follows a theme from the Bible.

  1. 9:00

    Welcome & games

    Name tags on, friends found, an ice-breaker game.

  2. 9:30

    Worship time

    Songs with actions, dancing and praise.

  3. 10:00

    Bible story

    The week’s story, told with drama, props and a memory verse.

  4. 10:45

    Snack break

    A healthy snack and drink, and a prayer of thanks.

  5. 11:15

    Character & life skills

    Small-group talk: kindness, honesty, saying sorry, making good choices.

  6. 11:45

    Crafts & prayer

    A craft to take home, then prayer for families and friends.

  7. 12:30

    Home time

    Handed over to parents, or home on our transport.
