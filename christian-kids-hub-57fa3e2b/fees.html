How to register

Four easy steps

  1. 1

    Send your details

    Use the registration form or WhatsApp: child’s name, age, school and home area.

  2. 2

    We confirm

    We call you back with the route, pick-up time or session details, and the fee.

  3. 3

    Meet the team

    Meet the driver, minder or trainer, and share who may collect your child.

  4. 4

    First day

    Your child joins in, and you get a WhatsApp message when they arrive.
