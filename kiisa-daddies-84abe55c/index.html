What are daddies?

Uganda’s favourite little crunch

Daddies are bite-sized cubes of sweet dough, folded into flaky layers and fried until golden and crunchy. Think of a mandazi’s crunchy little cousin — perfect on their own, with a cup of tea, or sprinkled over porridge.

At Kiisa Daddies, Sarah Kiisa makes every batch by hand, the way daddies should be made: good flour, a little spice, lots of patience — and no shortcuts.

  • Smallbatches, made fresh
  • Handfolded & cut
  • Sealedto stay crunchy

FAQ

Good questions

How long do daddies stay crunchy?

Kept sealed in a cool, dry place, our daddies stay crunchy for up to two weeks. Close the jar or bag tightly after opening.

What are the ingredients?

Wheat flour, sugar, margarine, a little spice and vegetable oil for frying. They contain wheat (gluten).

How much notice do you need?

Small orders can often be ready the same or next day. For events and bulk orders, please give us a few days’ notice.

Do you deliver?

Yes — tell us your area on WhatsApp and we’ll confirm delivery options and cost.
