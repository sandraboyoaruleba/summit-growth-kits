About Muwe

A building materials business that keeps its word.

Run by Joyce Kalule and a small team, Muwe Enterprise supplies the materials Ugandan homes, shops and walls are built from.

01 / Our story

From selling materials to supplying whole builds.

“We sell building materials physically. We are starting wholesaling to small hardware businesses, and we are starting selling gravel and sand and concrete bricks.”Joyce Kalule, in her own words

Muwe Enterprise began as a building materials business serving customers face to face. Today it is growing in three directions at once: concrete blocks, sand and gravel for builders, and wholesale supply for small hardware shops.

Joyce keeps a close eye on what sells, where and in which season, so the right materials are ready when builders need them.
