03 / How it works

Open an account in a day.

  1. Step 1

    Send your details

    Shop name, location and what you stock, on WhatsApp or the form.

  2. Step 2

    Get the price list

    Wholesale prices for the items you sell, confirmed for your area.

  3. Step 3

    First order

    We deliver to your shop and you check the load on arrival.
