08 / FAQ

Common questions.

Do you deliver to site?

Yes. We deliver blocks, sand and gravel to building sites and shops in our area. Delivery cost depends on distance and load size and is included in your quote.

What's the minimum order?

There's no minimum for collection. For delivery, it's most cost-effective to order a full load. Ask us and we'll advise.

How do wholesale accounts work?

Hardware shops can order at wholesale rates for bulk and repeat orders. Message us with your shop location and the items you stock and we'll set you up.

Why do sand and gravel prices change?

Prices for sand and aggregate move with season, fuel and quarry costs. We always quote today's price before you confirm.

Can I visit and see the materials?

Of course. Get in touch and we'll arrange a time for you to see the materials before you buy.
