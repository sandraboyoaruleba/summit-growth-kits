06 / FAQ

Common questions.

All questions
Do you deliver to site?

Yes. We deliver blocks, sand and gravel to building sites and shops in and around Kampala. Delivery cost depends on distance and load size and is included in your quote. See delivery & ordering.

What’s the minimum order?

There is no minimum if you collect. For delivery, a full load is the most cost-effective; for blocks we suggest at least 100. Ask us and we’ll advise.

How do wholesale accounts work?

Hardware shops order at wholesale rates for bulk and repeat orders. Message us with your shop location and what you stock, and we’ll set you up. See wholesale.

Why do sand and gravel prices change?

Sand and aggregate prices move with the season, fuel and quarry costs. We always quote today’s price before you confirm.
