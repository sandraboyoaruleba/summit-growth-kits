Delivery & ordering

Delivered to site, on time.

How ordering works, where we deliver, how to prepare your site and how to check your load.

Order nowWhatsApp

01 / Ordering

From message to delivery.

  1. Message us

    Materials, quantities, site location and when you need them, on WhatsApp or the quote form.

  2. Get your quote

    Today’s price with delivery included, usually the same day.

  3. Confirm & pay

    Mobile money, bank or cash. We confirm the delivery time.

  4. Receive & check

    Count and check the load before the truck leaves.

03 / Before the truck arrives

Prepare your site.

Clear access

A tipper needs firm ground and room to turn. Tell us about narrow roads, gates or soft ground.

Mark the spot

Choose where blocks and sand should go, close to where your builders will mix and lay.

Someone on site

Have someone there to receive, count and sign for the delivery.

04 / Honest loads

Check every delivery.

We want you to check. It is how trust is built.

  • Blocks: count the stacks before the truck leaves.
  • Sand & gravel: check the truck is full and the material is what you ordered.
  • Damage: set aside broken blocks and show the driver.
  • Anything wrong? WhatsApp us straight away and we put it right.
