Questions

About sand & gravel.

All questions
Why do sand and gravel prices change?

Sand and aggregate prices move with the season, fuel and quarry costs. We always quote today’s price before you confirm.

How much is in a trip?

It depends on the truck. We tell you the truck size and approximate volume with your quote, so you can compare fairly and check on arrival.

What if a delivery is short?

Count the blocks and check the load when it arrives, before the truck leaves. If anything is short or damaged, tell the driver and WhatsApp us at once, and we will make it right.

Can you bring cement and other items with my order?

Yes. Tell us what else you need (cement and site essentials) and we will quote it together, so it arrives in one delivery.

Need sand or gravel on site?

Tell us the job, the quantity and your location. We reply with today’s rate and the truck size.
