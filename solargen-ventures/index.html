Procedure for connection

Getting connected in 5 steps

Clear steps, published fees. No surprises.

  1. 1

    Apply

    Fill in an application form and attach:

    • Wiring certificate from a certified wireman
    • One passport photo
    • Copy of your national ID (both sides)
    • Letter from your village chairman proving land ownership
    • Wayleave form, if poles and wires pass through other people’s land
    • Trade licence (sole proprietors) · company seal & certificate of incorporation (companies)
  2. 2

    Pay inspection

    Pay the inspection charge when submitting your form.

    Single phaseUGX 38,000
    Three phaseUGX 80,000
  3. 3

    Site inspection

    A Solargen surveyor visits your premises to inspect the site.

  4. 4

    Pay connection fee

    After a successful inspection, pay the connection fee.

    No-pole serviceUGX 470,000
    One-pole serviceUGX 2,800,000
  5. 5

    Lights on!

    Your connection is done once the connection fee is received.

Fees as published by Solargen Ventures. Please confirm current charges with our office before paying. Electricity tariff: ask our team for the current unit price.

Our project

The Bussi Mini-grid

For years, residents of Bussi Island lived without grid electricity. The Bussi solar mini-grid changed that, bringing clean power to the island for the first time, built by Solargen with support from Canadian private donors.

Questions

Before you connect

Who can connect?

Homes, businesses and industries within reach of the Bussi mini-grid network. Send us your village and we’ll confirm coverage.

Single phase or three phase?

Single phase suits homes and most shops. Three phase is for heavier machines such as mills, welders and cold rooms. Our team will advise during inspection.

What is a “pole service”?

If your premises are next to the network you may need no new pole (UGX 470,000). If a new pole is required to reach you, the one-pole service applies (UGX 2,800,000).

How do I pay for electricity?

Electricity is purchased in advance using prepaid units via mobile money. Ask our team for details.
