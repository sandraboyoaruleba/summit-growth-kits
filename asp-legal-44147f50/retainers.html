Why a retainer

Small questions, answered before they grow

Most businesses only call a lawyer when something has already gone wrong. A retainer changes that: you can pick up the phone for a quick check on a clause, a staff issue or a letter from a regulator, without worrying about a new bill each time.

  • Predictable cost. One monthly fee you can budget for, agreed in writing.
  • Someone who knows your business. The same advocate, who learns your contracts, people and risks.
  • Faster answers. Retainer clients are first in line for calls and reviews.
  • Prevention over cure. Quarterly health checks catch gaps before they cost you.

Retainer questions

Before you sign up

All questions
Is there a minimum term?

We suggest an initial three months so we can learn your business; after that the retainer runs month to month with 30 days' notice.

What happens if we need more hours?

We tell you before going over. Extra work is billed at a preferential retainer rate agreed in your proposal, never as a surprise.

Does the retainer cover court cases?

Litigation is quoted separately because it depends on the case, but retainer clients receive preferential rates and we already know the background, which saves time and cost.

Can an NGO or school have a retainer?

Yes. Many organisations use the Business or Corporate plan for governance, staff and funder agreements.

How do we pay?

Monthly in advance by bank transfer or mobile money, against an invoice.
