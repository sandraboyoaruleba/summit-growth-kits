Getting started

How do I book a first consultation?

Use the consultation form or email us. Share a short summary of your matter (avoid sensitive details at this stage) and we will propose a time to meet at chambers or by video call.

How much does a consultation cost?

An initial consultation of up to one hour is UGX 150,000 (≈ US$41). If you go ahead with us on a fixed-fee task, we tell you whether the consultation fee is credited against it.

Is what I share confidential?

Yes. What you tell us in seeking advice is treated in strict confidence from the very first conversation, whether or not you go on to instruct us.

Can I have a consultation online?

Yes. Video consultations work well for clients outside Kampala and abroad. We send a secure link and you can share documents by email beforehand.

What should I bring to the first meeting?

Your ID, any documents you have (titles, agreements, letters, court papers), a short timeline and your questions. See what to bring.

Fees & payment

How are your fees structured?

Depending on the matter: a fixed fee (contracts, wills, searches, registrations), staged fees (cases and estates), hourly rates, or a monthly retainer for businesses. You always receive the fee in writing before work begins, exclusive of VAT and court or filing costs.

What is a retainer and do I need one?

A retainer gives your business ongoing access to legal advice for a predictable monthly fee. It suits companies and organisations that regularly deal with contracts, staff or compliance. See retainer plans.

Are court and registry fees included?

No. Government, registry, stamp duty and court fees are paid separately at cost, and we tell you about them in advance.

How do I pay?

By bank transfer or mobile money against an invoice. For larger matters, fees may be paid in agreed stages.

Working with us

Do you handle matters outside Kampala?

Yes. We advise clients across Uganda by video call and email, and attend to matters at courts and land offices outside Kampala by arrangement. Travel costs are agreed in advance.

How quickly will you reply?

We reply to new enquiries within one working day. Retainer clients have agreed response times, as fast as the same day.

Can you act if I live abroad?

Yes. We regularly support Ugandans abroad with land purchases, estates and family matters, by video call and email, and through powers of attorney where needed.

What if you cannot take my matter?

If we have a conflict of interest or the matter is outside our practice, we tell you promptly and, where we can, point you to the right kind of help.
