How we work

A clear path from question to resolution

Legal matters are stressful. Our process is designed to give you clarity at every step.

  1. Consultation

    We listen, review your documents and identify the real issues and what is at stake.

  2. Strategy & fees

    You receive clear options, our recommendation and an agreed fee before any work begins.

  3. Action

    We draft, negotiate or represent you, keeping you informed with regular updates.

  4. Resolution

    We close the matter properly and advise on how to stay protected going forward.

Questions

Frequently asked

How do I book a first consultation?

Use the form below or email us. Share a short summary of your matter and we’ll propose a time to meet in person or online.

How are your fees structured?

Depending on the matter, we work on agreed fixed fees, hourly rates or a monthly retainer. You will always receive a clear fee proposal before work begins.

Is what I share confidential?

Yes. Everything you share with us is protected by advocate–client confidentiality from the very first conversation.

What is a retainer and do I need one?

A retainer gives your business ongoing access to legal advice for a predictable monthly fee. It suits companies and organisations that regularly deal with contracts, staff or compliance.

Do you handle matters outside Kampala?

We advise clients across Uganda and can attend to matters in other jurisdictions within the country by arrangement.
