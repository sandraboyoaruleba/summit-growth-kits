How we work

A clear path from question to resolution

Legal matters are stressful. Our process gives you clarity at every step.

  1. Consultation

    We listen, review your documents and identify the real issues and what is at stake.

  2. Options & fee

    You receive clear options, our recommendation and a written fee before any work begins.

    We draft, negotiate or represent you, with regular updates in plain language.

    We close the matter properly and advise on how to stay protected going forward.

Questions

Good to know

What first-time clients ask us most.

All questionsAsk us directly
How do I book a first consultation?

Use the consultation form or email us. Share a short summary of your matter (avoid sensitive details at this stage) and we will propose a time to meet at chambers or by video call.

How much does a consultation cost?

An initial consultation of up to one hour is UGX 150,000 (≈ US$41). If you go ahead with us on a fixed-fee task, we tell you whether the consultation fee is credited against it.

Is what I share confidential?

Yes. What you tell us in seeking advice is treated in strict confidence from the very first conversation, whether or not you go on to instruct us.

How are your fees structured?

Depending on the matter: a fixed fee (contracts, wills, searches, registrations), staged fees (cases and estates), hourly rates, or a monthly retainer for businesses. You always receive the fee in writing before work begins, exclusive of VAT and court or filing costs.
