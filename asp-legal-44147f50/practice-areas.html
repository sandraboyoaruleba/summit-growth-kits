Corporate & Commercial

From the day you register a company to the day you sell a stake in it, the right paperwork protects what you are building. We advise founders, family businesses and established companies on structure, ownership and the decisions directors have to make.

How we help

  • Company and business name registration support (URSB)
  • Memorandum & articles, shareholder and partnership agreements
  • Board resolutions, minutes and annual compliance
  • Share transfers, new investors and joint ventures
  • Due diligence before you buy into or partner with a business

Fixed fee per task, or covered by a retainer
