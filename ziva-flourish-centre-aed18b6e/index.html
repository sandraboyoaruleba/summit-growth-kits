Meet our founder

Dr. Eunice Adubango (PhD)

“God has laid so much on my heart… Who walks with the widow? The widower? The child wondering why Jesus took mummy home? At Ziva Flourish Centre, we’re creating safe, healing spaces, where you will heal, learn, and thrive all around the pot.” Dr. Eunice Adubango
