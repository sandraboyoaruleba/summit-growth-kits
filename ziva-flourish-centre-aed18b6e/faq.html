FAQ

Questions? We’re glad you asked

Can’t find your answer here? Call or WhatsApp us on +256 770 491 804.

About Ziva

Is Ziva only about healing?

No. Ziva Flourish Centre is a centre for culinary arts and soul wellness. You can learn to cook and bake, receive Christian counselling, join planning and parenting programmes, or hire our spaces for events.

Is Ziva a Christian centre?

Yes. We are rooted in the love of Jesus Christ, and our counselling is faith-based. Everyone is welcome.

Where are you, and when are you open?

We are in Kasangati–Bulamu, Wakiso, Uganda, and open Monday to Saturday, 9am to 5pm. Call or WhatsApp +256 770 491 804 for directions.

Can I just come to rest, refresh and reset?

Yes, at a cost. You can book our meditation room by the hour.

Cookery & bakery classes

Do I need to book, or can I walk in?

You can walk in any day we are open. There are no rigid schedules. To be sure of a spot, or for a private or group session, send us a WhatsApp message first.

What will I learn in one class?

In a 2-hour class we teach you 3 items, and we pack variety into every session.

How much do classes cost?

Private sessions (just you) are priced per session, and group sessions are cheaper. Bakery and holiday Vacists classes are UGX 85,000 per session. WhatsApp us for current walk-in and group rates.

Do you teach children?

Yes. We run holiday Vacists classes for school children and P7 leavers, and we partner with schools for group sessions.

I live outside Uganda. Can I learn baking with you?

Please send us a message on WhatsApp or email thezivaflourish@gmail.com, and we will share the options available.

Counselling

How do I make an appointment for counselling?

Use our enquiry form, or call or WhatsApp +256 770 491 804. We will arrange a time that suits you.

Do you have sessions for couples?

Yes. We offer marriage, premarital and post-marital counselling for couples at every stage.

Are there online sessions for people who are upcountry or abroad?

Yes. Premarital and business counselling are available online. Write to us at thezivaflourish@gmail.com or WhatsApp us to arrange.

What does premarital counselling include?

It covers expectations, communication, financial management with practical tools, intimacy, and deliverance sessions, and it even includes cookery classes.

Domestic help training

What does the domestic help training cover?

It is a full 2-month programme, once a week. It covers all aspects of home care: cookery, snacks preparation, childcare, pet care, deliverance and therapy. It is home management, not cookery throughout. We follow through by checking past lessons, correcting mistakes and assigning homework.

Can the training happen at my home?

Yes. Training can take place at the centre or at your home. Transport is discussed based on your location.

Do you also supply or connect house helps?

No, we don’t. We train helpers that you bring (or yourself), but we do not supply or place house helps.

Events & space hire

What events can I host in the Flourish Lounge?

Parties, baby showers, bridal showers, meetings and fellowships. The lounge has power, water, washrooms and changing areas.

Can you provide food for my event?

Yes. You can bring your own meals, or let us handle the food. Tell us your date and number of guests and we’ll send you a quote.

How do I pay?

Contact us on +256 770 491 804 for payment details when you book.
