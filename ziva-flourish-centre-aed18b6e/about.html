About Ziva Flourish Centre

A sanctuary where faith meets wellness

Rooted in the love of Jesus Christ, we bring together the culinary arts (cookery and bakery) with soul care (counselling) to nurture the body, mind, and spirit.

Our story

More than a business: a God-led assignment

Ziva Flourish Centre opened its doors in Kasangati–Bulamu on 1 September 2025. It was born from a simple conviction: food is healing for the body, mind, and soul, and can be used as therapy.

We saw many people suffering in silence, without a safe space to process loss, pressure and pain. So we built one. It is a place where you can learn real skills in the kitchen, sit with a counsellor who feels like family, and plan a future with purpose.

Today the centre runs cookery and bakery classes, Christian counselling, domestic help training, planning and parenting programmes, and beautiful spaces for gatherings and quiet reflection.

The meaning of our name

Ziva: the Light of God

“Ziva is a Hebrew name which is a variant of Ziv and means radiance, brilliance, light, brightness and most importantly Light of God.”

Our flame is a reminder of that light, and of the cooking fire at the heart of our kitchen. We want every person who walks through our doors to flourish.

Meet our founder

Dr. Eunice Adubango (PhD)

  • Founder
  • Head Teaching Chef Instructor
  • 12+ years of culinary instruction
  • Speaker & teacher of the Word

Dr. Eunice Adubango is a chef, engineer, coach and mother. A former civil engineering lecturer at Makerere University, she established Ziva Flourish Centre to integrate practical culinary skills with “soul care”. The centre offers baking and cookery classes alongside counselling, mentorship and meditation spaces.

She is a guest teacher on the Hope Against Hope Show, where she speaks on marketplace spirituality, family and faith. In 2025 she was nominated for the Mkazipreneur Impact Awards in the Community / Social Impact category.

“Food is healing for the body, mind, and soul, and thus can be used as therapy.”Dr. Eunice Adubango
