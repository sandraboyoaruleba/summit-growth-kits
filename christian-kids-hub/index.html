School transport

From your gate to the school gate

One less thing for busy parents to worry about. Here's what a day with us looks like.

  1. 6:15am

    Morning pick-up

    We collect your child from an agreed pick-up point near home.

  2. On the road

    A calm, cheerful ride

    Seat belts on, a short prayer and songs on the way. No rushing, no overcrowding.

  3. 7:30am

    Safe drop-off

    Children are handed over at school, not just left at the gate.

  4. 5:00pm

    Home again

    Afternoon pick-up from school and drop-off back home to a waiting adult.

Our safety promise

  • Experienced, carefully selected drivers
  • A child minder on board
  • One seat, one child, seat belts on
  • Regular vehicle checks and servicing
  • WhatsApp updates for parents

Questions

Parents often ask

Something else on your mind? Ask us on WhatsApp.

How much does school transport cost?

Fees depend on your route and distance and are paid per term. Message us with your area and school for a quote.

Which schools and areas do you cover?

We cover routes in and around Kampala. Send us your location and school and we'll confirm.

What ages is the Christian training for?

Our sessions are designed for children aged about 4 to 13, grouped by age.

Do children need to use the transport to join the training?

No. Families can choose transport, Christian training or both.

Can our school partner with you?

Yes. We welcome schools looking for dependable transport or a Christian enrichment programme for pupils. Get in touch to talk it through.
