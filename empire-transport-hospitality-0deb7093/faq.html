Airport transfers

What if my flight is delayed?

Share your flight number when you book. We follow your arrival and adjust the pickup time, so a delay doesn’t cost you a missed ride.

Where will you meet me at the airport?

At the Entebbe International Airport arrivals exit, holding a board with your name. We send you a WhatsApp message when we are in position, with a photo of where we’re standing.

Do you pick up at night or very early in the morning?

Yes. Many flights land or leave late at night, so we work around your flight time, by booking.

How early should I leave Kampala for my flight?

For an international flight we usually suggest leaving central Kampala about 3–4 hours before departure. The Expressway is quick, but city traffic to reach it varies a lot. We’ll suggest a pickup time when you book.

How many passengers and bags can you take?

Our air-conditioned saloon or SUV takes up to 4 passengers with normal luggage. Tell us if you have extra bags or a larger group and we’ll plan for it.

Can you provide a child seat?

Ask when you book and we’ll confirm.

Booking & paying

How far in advance should I book?

As early as you can, ideally 24 hours ahead for airport runs. Same-day rides are often possible; just message us.

How do I pay?

Mobile Money or cash. The fare is agreed on WhatsApp before your trip, so there are no surprises.

Can I pay in US dollars?

Ask and we can quote in US dollars too. Our guide fares are in Ugandan shillings; dollar amounts on this site are approximate.

What if I need to cancel?

Let us know as early as possible on WhatsApp. There is no charge if you cancel well before the pickup time.

Can I get a receipt for my company?

Yes, ask and we’ll send a receipt with the company name.

Hotels, town runs & deliveries

Can you book my hotel too?

Yes. Tell us your dates, budget and the area you prefer, and we will recommend options and help with the reservation.

Is there a fee for hotel help?

Ask us. Recommendations are part of the service when you travel with us.

Can I keep the car and driver for the day?

Yes. Book a half day or full day with a driver for meetings, shopping or visiting family. See town runs.

What can you deliver?

Parcels, documents, shopping and gifts within Kampala and Entebbe. We don’t carry cash, dangerous goods or anything illegal. See home deliveries.

Do you travel outside Kampala and Entebbe?

Yes, by arrangement, for example a day trip to Jinja. Ask for a quote.
