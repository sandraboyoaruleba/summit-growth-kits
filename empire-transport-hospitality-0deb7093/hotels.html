Where to stay

Which part of town suits you?

A quick guide to the areas we’re asked about most. We’ll match the area to your plans and the traffic.

Entebbe

Close to the airport and Lake Victoria; quiet and green. Good for late arrivals and early flights.

Kololo & Nakasero

Central Kampala: embassies, offices, restaurants. Best for business trips.

Muyenga & Kansanga

Quieter hills with views, guesthouses and family homes. Good for longer stays.

Munyonyo & the lakeside

Lakeside resorts south of the city. Weekends, weddings and conferences.

Local advice

What we’ll ask you

  • Where are your meetings or family? Staying close saves hours in traffic.
  • When do you land and leave? A late arrival or early flight may suit Entebbe.
  • What matters most? Quiet, a pool, a gym, a kitchen, a view.
  • Your budget per night, in shillings or dollars.
