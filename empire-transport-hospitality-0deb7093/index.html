FAQ

Before you fly

All questions →
What if my flight is delayed?

Share your flight number when you book. We follow your arrival and adjust the pickup time, so a delay doesn’t cost you a missed ride.

Where will you meet me at the airport?

At the Entebbe International Airport arrivals exit, holding a board with your name. We send you a WhatsApp message when we are in position, with a photo of where we’re standing.

How far in advance should I book?

As early as you can, ideally 24 hours ahead for airport runs. Same-day rides are often possible; just message us.

Can you book my hotel too?

Yes. Tell us your dates, budget and the area you prefer, and we will recommend options and help with the reservation.

How do I pay?

Mobile Money or cash. The fare is agreed on WhatsApp before your trip, so there are no surprises.
