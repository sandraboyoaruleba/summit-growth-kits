FAQ

Before you fly

Can’t see your question? Message us any time on WhatsApp.

What if my flight is delayed?

Share your flight number when booking. We follow your arrival and adjust the pickup time automatically.

Where will you meet me at the airport?

At the Entebbe International Airport arrivals exit, holding a board with your name. We send you a WhatsApp message when we are in position.

How far in advance should I book?

As early as you can, ideally 24 hours ahead. Same-day rides are often possible; just message us.

Can you book my hotel too?

Yes. Tell us your dates, budget and the area you prefer, and we will recommend options and help with the reservation.

How do I pay?

Mobile Money or cash. The fare is agreed on WhatsApp before your trip.
