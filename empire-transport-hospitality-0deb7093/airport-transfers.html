Your arrival, step by step

From the aircraft door to yours

Arriving in a new country after a long flight is tiring. Here is exactly what happens, so you know what to expect.

  1. 1

    Before you fly

    Send your flight number, number of passengers and bags, and where you’re going. We confirm the fare on WhatsApp.

  2. 2

    Wheels down

    We follow your flight. While you clear immigration and collect bags, we’re already in position.

  3. 3

    Arrivals exit

    Look for your name on our board as you walk out. We’ll have messaged you a photo of the spot.

  4. 4

    On the road

    Water, phone charging, a stop for cash or a SIM card if you need one, just ask.

  5. 5

    At your door

    We carry your bags in and make sure you’re checked in or home safe. Family abroad can be updated too.

Drop-offs

Leaving Uganda? Leave the timing to us.

The Kampala–Entebbe Expressway is quick, but getting to it can take longer than you expect. We plan your pickup around your flight time and the traffic of the day.

  • International flights: we usually suggest leaving central Kampala 3–4 hours before departure.
  • Regional flights: about 2–3 hours before.
  • Night and early-morning flights: we work around your flight time, by booking.

Airport questions

Good to know

All questions →
What if my flight is delayed?

Share your flight number when you book. We follow your arrival and adjust the pickup time, so a delay doesn’t cost you a missed ride.

Where will you meet me at the airport?

At the Entebbe International Airport arrivals exit, holding a board with your name. We send you a WhatsApp message when we are in position, with a photo of where we’re standing.

Do you pick up at night or very early in the morning?

Yes. Many flights land or leave late at night, so we work around your flight time, by booking.

How early should I leave Kampala for my flight?

For an international flight we usually suggest leaving central Kampala about 3–4 hours before departure. The Expressway is quick, but city traffic to reach it varies a lot. We’ll suggest a pickup time when you book.

How many passengers and bags can you take?

Our air-conditioned saloon or SUV takes up to 4 passengers with normal luggage. Tell us if you have extra bags or a larger group and we’ll plan for it.

Can you provide a child seat?

Ask when you book and we’ll confirm.
