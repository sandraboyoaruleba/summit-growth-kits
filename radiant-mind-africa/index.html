Questions

Good to know

Can't find your answer? Send us a message.

Is the discovery call really free?

Yes. It's a no-obligation conversation to understand your situation and recommend the right path, whether that's coaching, a programme or something else.

Do you work online or in person?

Both. We work with clients across Africa and globally online, and deliver in-person sessions and workshops where it makes sense.

Can you design a programme for our organisation?

Yes. Our advisory model is built around custom solutions: leadership training, change management, team effectiveness and learning pathways tailored to your context.

Which programme is right for me?

If you feel stuck mid-career, start with MiCR. New or senior leaders often fit LiFT; early-career Gen Z leaders, AKILI NextGen; aspiring founders, the Radiant Venture Lab. We'll help you choose on the discovery call.

Do you offer speaking engagements?

Yes. Blenda speaks on career transition, resilience, mindset and leadership. Use the form to share your event details.
