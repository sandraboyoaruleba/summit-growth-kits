Our story

A neighbourhood kitchen that grew with Kyebando

Momoz Oven Cafe was built by Arinaitwe Moreen around a simple idea: generous continental food, cooked fresh and served with real warmth — at prices that feel fair.

Today our team of more than fifty people serves dine-in guests on the ground floor of Akamwesi Shopping Mall, takes away for busy commuters on Gayaza Road, and delivers across Kampala. Whether it’s a quick shawarma, a family sizzler or a birthday platter, every plate leaves our oven with the same care.

  • Dine in, take away or delivery
  • Breakfast from 9am, dinner until late
  • Platters for groups, meetings & celebrations

Good to know

Questions

Where exactly are you?

On the ground floor of Akamwesi Shopping Mall in Kyebando, along Gayaza Road — easy to reach from Gayaza Road, the Kyebando ring road and the Northern Bypass.

Do you deliver?

Yes. Order on WhatsApp or by phone, or use Glovo. Delivery fees depend on your location — we’ll confirm when you order.

Can you cater for a group or event?

Our 2, 4 and 6-person mixed platters are made for sharing. For bigger celebrations or office lunches, send us your numbers on WhatsApp and we’ll put together a package.

How can I pay?

Cash, Mobile Money (MTN & Airtel) and card.
