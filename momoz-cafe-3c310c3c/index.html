Our story

A neighbourhood kitchen that grew with Kyebando

Momoz Oven Cafe was built by Arinaitwe Moreen around a simple idea: generous continental food, cooked fresh and served with real warmth, at prices that feel fair.

“I want every guest to feel at home the moment they sit down, and to leave already planning what they will order next time.”Arinaitwe Moreen, founder

Today a team of more than fifty people cooks, serves and delivers from the ground floor of Akamwesi Shopping Mall.
