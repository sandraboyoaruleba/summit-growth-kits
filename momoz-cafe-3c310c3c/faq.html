Questions, answered

Hours, the menu, vegetarian options, delivery, payment and group bookings: the things guests ask us most.

Can’t find your question? Ask us on WhatsApp.

Visiting

Where exactly are you?

On the ground floor of Akamwesi Shopping Mall in Kyebando, on Gayaza Road, Kampala. The mall is easy to reach from Gayaza Road, the Kyebando ring road and the Northern Bypass. See the map.

What are your opening hours?

Monday to Thursday 9:00am to 10:00pm, and Friday to Sunday 9:00am to 11:00pm.

Do I need to book a table?

Walk-ins are always welcome. For groups, birthdays and weekend evenings (our busiest time), booking ahead on WhatsApp is a good idea. Weekdays are usually quieter.

Do you have outdoor seating?

Yes, there is indoor and outdoor seating.

Is there parking?

Akamwesi Shopping Mall has parking for shoppers and guests.

Food & menu

Where can I see the menu and prices?

The full menu is on this site with prices in Ugandan shillings (UGX). Prices come from our Glovo menu; in-store prices may differ slightly.

Do you have vegetarian options?

Yes: Vegetable Biryani, Margarita pizza, Spaghetti Napolitana, Vegetable Sandwich, vegetable and mushroom soups, waffles, pancakes, milkshakes and organic juices. Tell us about any allergy when you order.

Can I ask for my food a certain way?

Of course. Shawarma extra juicy, less sauce, mild instead of spicy: say so when you order and we’ll cook it your way.

Is the meat halal?

Please ask the team when you order and we’ll tell you about each dish.

Do you make cakes to order?

Yes. Tell us the date, size and message for a celebration cake and we’ll confirm the price. See sweets & shakes.

Ordering & delivery

How do I order for delivery?

Use the order form, which writes the WhatsApp message for you, or message us on WhatsApp at +256 783 329 021, or call +256 703 198 287. You can also order on Glovo.

Which areas do you deliver to, and how much is delivery?

We deliver around Kyebando and north Kampala. The fee depends on distance and is confirmed before we start cooking.

How can I pay?

Cash, and Mobile Money (MTN & Airtel). Ask us about card payments in the cafe.

Can you cater for a group, office or party?

Yes. Our 2, 4 and 6-person platters are made for sharing, and we can put together pizzas, shawarma and drinks for office lunches. See platters & parties.

Hungry? We’ll have it ready.

Send your order on WhatsApp and we confirm the total, the time and any delivery fee. Or walk in: the oven is on from 9am.
