How it started

Good food, great hosts

Momoz Oven Cafe was built by Arinaitwe Moreen around a simple idea: continental food that’s generous, freshly cooked and fairly priced, served by people who are glad you came.

From the oven came pizzas and bakes; from the kitchen, sizzlers, curries, pilau and shawarma; and from the dessert counter, the ice cream rolls that have become a Momoz signature. Guests could sit down, take away or have it delivered.

That idea grew. Today a team of more than fifty people cooks, serves and delivers, and tens of thousands of people follow the kitchen on TikTok.

The name

Why “Oven Cafe”?

Because the oven is where Momoz starts: pizzas, bakes and cakes come out of it all day, from the first breakfast at 9am to the last order at night.
