Our story

Small acorns. Mighty oaks.

Oak International Christian School was founded in 2015 to provide an educational experience that would nurture transformational leaders who would impact their communities, the nation and the world for God.

That mandate is grounded on the authority of the Bible and carried out through Christian education using the Accelerated Christian Education (ACE) curriculum. Every day, our teachers help children build strong foundations in reading, numbers and character — in a safe, joyful environment where each child is known by name.

What is ACE?

Learning that fits each child

The A.C.E. programme is an individualised, self-instructional, Bible-based K–12 curriculum. It provides all the basic academic disciplines while imparting godly values and character in a God-centred environment of absolutes and love. ACE has been used around the world for over 50 years.

  • 🧩

    Individualised

    Each child starts at their true level and moves forward as they master each step.

  • 🎯

    Mastery-based

    Children achieve high scores before progressing — no gaps left behind.

  • ✝️

    Bible-based

    Scripture and character lessons are built into every subject.

  • 🌍

    Recognised worldwide

    A curriculum used by Christian schools across the world.

Parents ask

Frequently asked questions

What ages do you teach?

We educate children from 3 to 12 years — nursery through primary.

What curriculum do you use?

We use Accelerated Christian Education (ACE): an individualised, self-instructional, Bible-based curriculum used worldwide for over 50 years.

Where is the school?

Along Kira – Kasangati Road, Buwate, Kira Municipality, Wakiso District.

Do you offer transport and meals?

Yes — ask us about school transport routes and the daily meal plan.

Can my child join mid-year?

Because ACE is individualised, children can usually join at any time and start at the right level after a short diagnostic assessment.
