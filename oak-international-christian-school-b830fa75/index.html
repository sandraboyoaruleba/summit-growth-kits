Learning at Oak

A joyful journey from age 3 to 12

Two stages, one caring school: strong foundations in kindergarten, then individualised ACE learning through primary.

Ages 3 – 5

Kindergarten

Play-based learning that builds early reading, numbers and social skills, with Bible stories, songs, art and plenty of outdoor fun.

  • Step by Step class (3 years)
  • Preschool (4 years)
  • Phonics, counting & memory verses
Explore kindergarten
Ages 6 – 12

Primary

Each child works through ACE PACEs at their own level, mastering every step before moving on, with daily goals and teacher support.

  • Maths, English, Word Building
  • Science, Social Studies & Bible
  • Daily goals & mastery
Explore primary

A day at Oak

What a happy day looks like

Learning, worship, play and friendship, in a calm and caring rhythm.

  1. 7:30

    Warm welcome

    Arrival, greetings and settling in with friends.

  2. 8:00

    Morning devotion

    Praise, prayer and a Bible story to start the day.

  3. 8:30

    Learning time

    Focused work on PACEs and early-years activities with teacher support.

  4. 10:30

    Break & play

    Snacks, fresh air and outdoor games.

  5. 12:30

    Lunch

    A nourishing meal together.

  6. 2:00

    Creative afternoon

    Art, music, reading, sports and clubs.

Times are a guide; confirm the current timetable with the school office.

What ages do you teach?

Children from 3 to 12 years: kindergarten through primary.

What curriculum do you use?

Accelerated Christian Education (ACE): an individualised, self-instructional, Bible-based curriculum used worldwide for over 50 years. How ACE works.

Can my child join mid-year?

Usually, yes. Because ACE is individualised, children can start at the right level after a short placement test.
