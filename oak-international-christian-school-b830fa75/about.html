Mission

Nurturing transformational leaders

Oak International Christian School was founded in 2015 to provide an educational experience that would nurture transformational leaders who would impact their communities, the nation and the world for God.

That mandate is grounded on the authority of the Bible and carried out through Christian education using the Accelerated Christian Education (ACE) curriculum. Every day, our teachers help children build strong foundations in reading, numbers and character, in a safe, joyful environment where each child is known by name.

Why “Oak”?

Deep roots, strong branches

An oak begins as a small acorn and, with time, care and deep roots, becomes a tree that shelters others. That is our picture of every child at Oak: rooted in God’s Word, growing in knowledge, and one day giving shade and strength to their community.

Our crest shows an oak tree with a cross at its heart, and a little sun: faith at the centre, and a bright future ahead.

“That they might be called trees of righteousness, the planting of the Lord, that he might be glorified.” Isaiah 61:3

Our team

Teachers who know every child

A team of 11–20 teachers and staff serves our children. In ACE classrooms, supervisors guide each child’s daily goals and monitors help children as they work, so help is always close.

  • Christian teachers who model the values they teach
  • Teachers trained in the ACE approach
  • Small classes and individual attention
“We don’t just teach lessons. We walk with each child, one goal at a time, until they can stand tall on their own.”Robert Kibirango, Oak International Christian School
