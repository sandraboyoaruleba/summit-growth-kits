About the school

What ages do you teach?

Children from 3 to 12 years: kindergarten (Step by Step, Preschool and K5) and primary.

Where is the school?

Along Kira – Kasangati Road in Buwate, Kira Municipality, Wakiso District. See the map.

Are you the same as Oak International School in Kampala?

No. That is a different school that follows the Cambridge curriculum. Oak International Christian School is a Christian school using the ACE curriculum in Buwate, Kira. On Facebook we appear as OAK International Christian Academy.

Is the school Christian?

Yes. Oak is Christ-centred: the Bible is the foundation of our mission, our curriculum and our daily devotion. Families of all backgrounds are welcome to enquire.

Learning & ACE

What curriculum do you use?

Accelerated Christian Education (ACE): an individualised, self-instructional, Bible-based curriculum used worldwide for over 50 years. How ACE works.

How will I know my child is progressing?

Teachers track daily goals and PACE test results, and parents receive regular progress reports.

Do pupils sit the Ugandan PLE?

Please ask the school office how Oak pupils move on after primary, including ICCE and national exam options.

What happens after age 12?

ACE continues into secondary, and ACE graduates receive ICCE certificates. Ask us about current options after primary.

Admissions & fees

Can my child join mid-year?

Usually yes. A short placement test finds the right ACE level, so children can join at any time.

What are the fees?

As a guide: kindergarten from UGX 450,000 per term (≈ US$120), primary from UGX 650,000 per term (≈ US$175). Sample fees for this preview. See the fees guide.

What do I need for admission?

A birth certificate, the latest school report, two passport photos and parent contact details.

Daily life

Do you offer transport and meals?

Yes. Ask us about school transport routes and the daily meal plan.

What are the school hours?

Children arrive from 7:30am. Kindergarten finishes around 12:30pm and primary in the afternoon.

Can I visit before applying?

Of course. Book a visit on any school day by WhatsApp or through the contact form.
