FAQ

Questions, answered

Who do you work with?

Small and growing businesses, solo entrepreneurs and busy professionals who need dependable support without hiring full-time staff.

Do you work remotely or in person?

Both. Admin, marketing and customer support can be done remotely; errands and some personal assistance are done on the ground in Kampala and nearby areas.

How do I pay?

Mobile Money or bank transfer. Errand purchases are agreed upfront.

How quickly can you start?

Most single tasks can begin as soon as scope and price are agreed. Monthly plans usually start with a short onboarding call.

Is my business information safe?

Yes. We treat your accounts, documents and customer data as confidential, and can sign a simple confidentiality agreement on request.
