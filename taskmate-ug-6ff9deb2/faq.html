Working with us

What kind of tasks can I hand over?

Administration, digital marketing, social media management, customer support, errands and personal assistance. If it is on your to-do list and slowing you down, ask us.

Do you work with small businesses?

Yes. Most of our clients are small and growing businesses: shops, salons, cafés, consultants and online sellers.

Can I hire you for just one task?

Yes. Single tasks start from UGX 20,000. See plans & prices.

I live abroad. Can you help with my business in Uganda?

Yes. We can be your eyes and hands in Uganda: payments, follow-ups, site and staff check-ins, all reported on WhatsApp with photos and receipts.

Trust & confidentiality

Is my information kept confidential?

Yes. We only access what a task needs, and we are happy to sign a confidentiality agreement.

Do I need to share my passwords?

Where possible we use shared access (for example, adding us as a page admin) so you never share passwords. You can remove access at any time.

How do I know the work was done?

You get a short report after each task with proof: screenshots, receipts, photos or the finished file.

Prices & payment

How do you price your work?

Per task for one-off jobs, or a monthly plan for ongoing work. You always get the price before we start.

How do I pay?

Mobile Money or bank transfer. Monthly plans are paid at the start of the month.

Who pays for transport and ad budgets?

Errand transport is agreed in advance and added to the errand fee. Ad budgets are paid by you directly to the platform.

Practical

What are your working hours?

Mon – Sat · 8:00am – 7:00pm. WhatsApp messages outside these hours are answered the next working day.

Which areas do you cover for errands?

Kampala and nearby areas. Ask us about trips further out.
