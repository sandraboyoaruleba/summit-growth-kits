About TaskmateUg

Your task mate in Uganda

TaskmateUg helps business owners with administration, digital marketing, social media, customer support, errands and personal assistance. We are a small team of two to five people, led by Tiba Success and a business partner.

  • Team of 2–5
  • Kampala, Uganda
  • WhatsApp-first

Our story

Built for owners who do it all

In Uganda, most business owners are the boss, the accountant, the marketer, the customer service desk and the delivery person, all at once. The work that grows the business gets pushed to tomorrow.

TaskmateUg was started by Tiba Success and a business partner to take that load off. We handle the tasks that drain your time, from replying to customers and posting on Instagram to sending invoices and picking up stock, so you can focus on the work only you can do.

We work with salons, shops, restaurants, consultants and diaspora owners who run businesses back home.
