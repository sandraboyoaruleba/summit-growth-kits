Referring a child

Three simple steps to refer

Paediatricians, family doctors, therapists, teachers and community workers are all welcome to refer.

  1. 1

    Talk to the family

    Explain why you suggest a developmental review, and get their consent to share information.

  2. 2

    Send a short letter

    Email cnerima@yahoo.com with the child’s age, your concern, and any reports. Or give the family our details.

  3. 3

    We close the loop

    We contact the family to book, and (with their consent) send you a summary and plan after the assessment.

Helpful to include

What makes a good referral

  • Child’s name, date of birth and parent’s contact
  • Your main concern, in a sentence or two
  • Relevant history: prematurity, illness, seizures, hearing or vision
  • What has been tried, and any therapy already in place
  • Copies of reports, scans or school notes

Please do not send urgent or emergency cases by email. A child who is acutely unwell should go to the nearest hospital.

What you can refer for

  • Developmental delay
  • Suspected autism
  • Cerebral palsy
  • Speech & language delay
  • ADHD & learning
  • Behaviour concerns
  • Genetic conditions
  • Follow-up of preterm babies
  • Second opinions

Not sure if a child is right for Atamba? Email and ask. A quick word is always welcome.
