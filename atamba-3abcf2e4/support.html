Caregiver Skills Training

Learning from a master trainer

Dr. Caroline is a master trainer for Caregiver Skills Training (CST) in Uganda, a programme developed by the World Health Organization and supported by UNICEF for families of children with developmental delays or disabilities, including autism.

Caregivers learn everyday ways to engage their child in play and home routines, build communication, and handle difficult behaviour calmly, with practice at home and support from the trainer.

  • Joining in your child’s play and routines
  • Building communication, one step at a time
  • Preventing and handling challenging behaviour
  • Looking after yourself as a caregiver

After the assessment

Your first three months

A typical path for a family after a first assessment. Every plan is different, and we go at your pace.

Simple toys and everyday games are some of the best therapy tools there are.

  1. Week 1

    Your summary and plan

    Two or three clear priorities, and what each person at home can do.

  2. Weeks 2–4

    Getting started

    Therapy referrals made, school informed if you wish, and a first coaching session.

  3. Weeks 4–10

    Practice at home

    Ten to fifteen minutes a day of play-based activities. Questions answered by email.

  4. Week 12

    Review

    We look at progress together and adjust the plan.

Everyday ideas

Small things that help every child

General ideas any family can try today. Your own plan will be tailored to your child.

Talk about now

Name what your child is looking at or doing. Short, simple words, many times a day.

Follow their lead

Join the game your child chooses. Copy them, then add one small new step.

Same rhythm daily

Predictable routines for meals, bath and bed help children feel safe and cope better.

Less screen, more play

Swap some screen time for face-to-face play, songs and books together.
