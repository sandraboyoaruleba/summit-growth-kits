What is a developmental paediatrician?

A children’s doctor with extra training in how children grow, move, communicate, learn and behave. It is still a new specialty in Uganda, and families no longer have to travel far to find it.

Is my child on track?

Trust your instinct. It’s okay to ask.

Children develop at different speeds, and many worries turn out to be nothing. But early support makes a real difference. If any of these sound familiar, an assessment brings clarity.

0 – 2 years

Babies & toddlers

  • Not sitting, crawling or walking around the expected ages
  • Few smiles, little eye contact, not answering to their name
  • No babbling by 12 months, no words by 16–18 months
  • Losing skills they once had

2 – 5 years

Young children

  • Speech that is hard to understand, or not joining words
  • Prefers to play alone, repetitive play, very strong routines
  • Frequent falls, stiffness or unusual movement
  • Big struggles with sleep, feeding or behaviour

School age

6 years and up

  • Struggling to read, write or keep up despite effort
  • Trouble focusing, sitting still or following instructions
  • Difficulty making friends or reading social cues
  • Teachers raising the same concern again and again

    Reach out

    Email us (WhatsApp coming soon) with your child’s age and your main concern. A referral letter is welcome but not required.

All questionsAsk us
Do I need a referral?

No. Many families come through their doctor, but you are welcome to contact us directly. If you have a referral letter or earlier reports, please bring them.

What ages do you see?

Babies, children and teenagers from birth to 18 years. The earlier a concern is checked, the more time there is to help.

Will my child get a diagnosis?

Sometimes a diagnosis is clear at the first visit; sometimes we need more time, more observations or tests. Either way, you leave with practical next steps.

How much does it cost?

A first developmental assessment is UGX 350,000 (≈ US$95) (sample fee). The fee is always confirmed before you book. See all fees.
