Meet your doctor

Dr. Caroline Nerima

Paediatrician & developmental paediatrician · Founder of Atamba

Dr. Nerima has spent her career with children and the people who love them — in general paediatrics, in HIV care programmes, and in the assessment and management of children with developmental delay and disability. She completed specialist clinical training in Developmental Paediatrics at Red Cross War Memorial Children’s Hospital in Cape Town, one of Africa’s leading children’s hospitals.

She also trains others: health workers, Village Health Teams and teachers across Uganda have learned to spot and support developmental needs through programmes she has led.

How we help

Care that sees the whole child

For schools, clinics & organisations

Training for teachers, health workers and community teams on early identification of developmental needs and inclusive support.

Is my child on track?

Trust your instinct. It’s okay to ask.

Children develop at different speeds, and many worries turn out to be nothing. But early support makes a real difference, so if any of these sound familiar, an assessment can bring clarity and peace of mind.

Talk to Dr. Nerima
Babies & toddlers (0–2 years)
  • Not sitting, crawling or walking around the expected ages
  • Few smiles, little eye contact or not responding to their name
  • No babbling by 12 months, or no words by 16–18 months
  • Losing skills they once had
Young children (2–5 years)
  • Speech that is hard to understand, or not joining words
  • Prefers to play alone, repetitive play or strong routines
  • Frequent falls, stiffness or unusual movement
  • Big difficulties with sleep, feeding or behaviour
School-age children
  • Struggling to read, write or keep up despite effort
  • Trouble focusing, sitting still or following instructions
  • Difficulty making friends or understanding social cues
  • Teachers raising repeated concerns

This list is a guide, not a diagnosis. If you are worried, please reach out.

Your first visit

What to expect, step by step

  1. 1

    Reach out

    Send a message or email with your child’s age and your main concern. Referral letters are welcome but not required.

  2. 2

    Getting to know you

    A relaxed conversation about pregnancy, birth, milestones, health, family and what daily life looks like.

  3. 3

    Play-based assessment

    Your child plays, moves and talks while Dr. Nerima observes. You stay with your child throughout.

  4. 4

    A clear plan

    Findings explained in plain words, a written summary, next steps, referrals and follow-up you can count on.

Questions parents ask

FAQ

Can’t find your answer? Send us a message.

Do I need a referral?

No. Many families come through their doctor, but you are welcome to contact us directly. If you have a referral letter or previous reports, please bring them.

What ages do you see?

Babies, children and teenagers from birth to 18 years.

How long is an assessment?

A first assessment usually takes about 60–90 minutes, so your child has time to settle and play.

What does it cost?

Fees depend on the type of assessment. Please ask when booking and we will explain clearly before your visit.

Will my child get a diagnosis?

Sometimes a diagnosis is clear at the first visit; sometimes we need more time, observations or tests. Either way, you will leave with practical next steps.

Do you work with schools and therapists?

Yes. With your permission, we share reports and work together with teachers, therapists and other doctors so that everyone supports your child in the same direction.
