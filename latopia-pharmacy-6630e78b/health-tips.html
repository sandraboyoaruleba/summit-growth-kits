Health tips from your pharmacy

Little habits, healthier you

Friendly, practical advice for everyday health, from malaria to skincare. For anything serious, always see a doctor.

  • 8 practical guides
  • Questions welcome
  • For the whole family
Malaria

Test before you treat malaria

Not every fever is malaria. Uganda's health guidelines say to test before treating, so you take the right medicine.

  • Get tested at the first sign of fever
  • If positive, finish the full course, even if you feel better
  • Sleep under a treated mosquito net every night
  • Children under five and pregnant women need extra care
Medicines

Finish your antibiotics

Stopping early when you feel better can let an infection come back stronger and harder to treat.

  • Take antibiotics only with a prescription
  • Finish the course exactly as directed
  • Never share antibiotics or save leftovers
  • Antibiotics don't work on colds and flu (viruses)
Heart health

Check your blood pressure

High blood pressure is silent. A two-minute check at the pharmacy can protect your heart and brain.

  • Adults: check at least once a year
  • Cut down on salt and processed food
  • Move for 30 minutes most days
  • If you're on BP medicine, don't stop it without advice
Children

Diarrhoea in children: ORS and zinc

Diarrhoea drains water and salts from a child's body fast. ORS replaces them, and zinc helps recovery.

  • Give ORS in small, frequent sips
  • Give zinc daily for the full 10–14 days, as advised
  • Keep breastfeeding and offering food
  • Go to a clinic for blood in stool, sunken eyes or a child who won't drink
Skin

Sunscreen isn't only for holidays

Daily SPF helps prevent dark spots and uneven tone, whatever your skin colour.

  • Apply every morning, even when it's cloudy
  • Use enough to cover face, ears and neck
  • Reapply after sweating or washing
  • Ask us for a sunscreen that suits your skin
Safety

Store medicines safely at home

Heat, damp and curious children are the main risks to medicines at home.

  • Keep medicines cool, dry and out of direct sun
  • Store them up high, away from children
  • Keep them in the original pack with the label
  • Check expiry dates and bring old medicines to us
Pregnancy

Planning a pregnancy? Start folic acid

Folic acid helps protect your baby's spine and brain in the earliest weeks, often before you know you're pregnant.

  • Ask about folic acid when planning a pregnancy
  • Attend antenatal visits early and regularly
  • Check with us before taking any medicine in pregnancy
  • Iron and folic acid as advised by your health worker
Diabetes

Know the signs of diabetes

Diabetes often starts quietly. Catching it early prevents serious problems with eyes, kidneys and feet.

  • Signs: thirst, passing urine often, tiredness, slow-healing wounds
  • Check your sugar if you're over 40 or overweight
  • Choose water over sugary drinks
  • Look after your feet and check them daily if diabetic

This is general information, not personal medical advice. Ask us or your doctor about your own situation.
