Prescriptions made easy

Send it, we prepare it

  1. Snap your prescription

    Take a clear photo of the whole prescription and send it on WhatsApp, with your name and phone number.

  2. We check and confirm

    We check availability and price, and let you know if a generic option can save you money.

  3. Collect with the original

    Pick up at PJK House and bring the original prescription. We'll explain the dose before you go.

Prescription-only medicines (such as antibiotics) need a valid prescription from a doctor or clinician. Over-the-counter medicines, beauty products and supplements don't.

Questions worth asking

We're happy to answer these every time, so you get the most from your medicine.

  • Should I take this with food, or on an empty stomach?
  • Can I take it with my other medicines or supplements?
  • Is it safe in pregnancy or while breastfeeding?
  • What side effects should I watch for?
  • What if I miss a dose?

When to see a doctor, not just a pharmacy. Go to a clinic or hospital straight away for: a very high fever in a young child, fits or confusion, difficulty breathing, chest pain, severe bleeding, a baby who won't feed, or symptoms that keep getting worse. In an emergency call 112.
