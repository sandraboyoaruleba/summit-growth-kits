A simple routine

Three steps, morning and night

  1. Cleanse

    A gentle cleanser removes dirt and sweat without stripping your skin.

  2. Moisturise

    Even oily skin needs moisture. Choose a light lotion or a richer cream to suit you.

  3. Protect

    Sunscreen every morning helps prevent dark spots and uneven tone, even on cloudy days.

Be careful with skin-lightening creams. Some contain mercury or strong steroids that can damage skin. Ask us before you buy, and use medicated creams only as advised by a health worker.
