Getting started

Is the discovery call really free?

Yes. It’s a no-obligation, confidential conversation of about 30 minutes to understand your situation and recommend the right path, whether that’s coaching, a programme or something else.

Which programme is right for me?

If you feel stuck mid-career, start with the Mid-Career Reinvention Circle (MiCR). New or senior leaders often fit LiFT; early-career Gen Z leaders, AKILI NextGen; aspiring founders, the Radiant Venture Lab. We’ll help you choose on the discovery call.

Do you work online or in person?

Both. We work with clients across Africa and globally online, and deliver in-person sessions and workshops in Kampala and where it makes sense.

I live outside Uganda. Can I join?

Yes. Coaching and most programme sessions can be joined online. Fees are shown in Ugandan shillings with approximate US dollar amounts; ask us for an international quote.

Programmes & coaching

When does the next cohort start?

New cohorts open throughout the year. Send us a message and we’ll share the next intake dates for the programme you’re interested in.

How big are the cohorts?

Small enough for everyone to be heard, usually 8 to 15 people.

What is the difference between coaching and a programme?

Coaching is one-to-one and shaped entirely around you. Programmes are structured journeys with a peer community, tools like AURA and the Life Capital Model, and continued support after the sessions end.

Is coaching the same as counselling or therapy?

No. Coaching focuses on where you are now and where you want to go: clarity, decisions, habits and leadership. If something deeper comes up, we’ll encourage you to see a qualified counsellor or therapist.

Is what I share confidential?

Yes. What you share in coaching stays between you and your coach. For organisation-sponsored coaching, we agree upfront what (if anything) is reported back.

Can I pay in instalments?

Ask us; for longer programmes, payment can often be spread.

Organisations & speaking

Can you design a programme for our organisation?

Yes. Our advisory model is built around custom solutions: leadership training, change management, team effectiveness and learning pathways tailored to your context.

Which sectors do you work with?

Blenda brings over 15 years of experience across corporate, government and international development sectors.

Do you offer speaking engagements?

Yes. Blenda speaks on career transition, resilience, mindset and leadership. Use the booking form to share your event details.

How is organisational work priced?

Every engagement is quoted after a scoping conversation, based on team size, scope and format. There is no charge for the first conversation.
