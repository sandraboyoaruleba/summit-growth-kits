How an engagement works

Diagnose. Design. Deliver. Sustain.

We don’t parachute in a generic workshop. We start by understanding what’s really happening in your organisation.

  1. 1

    Scoping call

    A free conversation with your leadership or HR team about the change you’re facing.

  2. 2

    Diagnose

    Interviews, short surveys and the AURA lens to see where alignment is breaking down.

  3. 3

    Design

    A proposal tailored to your context: format, timeline, people involved and outcomes.

  4. 4

    Deliver

    Workshops, cohorts, coaching and advisory, in person or online.

  5. 5

    Sustain

    Follow-up, learning pathways and check-ins so the change sticks.

The method

AURA for teams

“Leadership is not a title. It is a process.” From the #BeyondTheShift series on the Radiant Mind Africa blog.

A

Awareness

Seeing clearly: understand your current reality and your own triggers honestly.

U

Unification

Aligning the “inner spine”: integrate identity, values and goals.

R

Resonance

Leading effectively in relationship, especially under pressure.

A

Amplification

Scaling impact through systems: purposeful action that lasts.
