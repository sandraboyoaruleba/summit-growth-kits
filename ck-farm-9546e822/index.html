Better together

Why we grow coffee under bananas

Shade

Banana leaves protect young coffee from harsh sun and heat.

Soil

Fallen leaves and stems become mulch, keeping moisture in and feeding the ground.

Steady income

Bananas give harvests all year while coffee follows its seasons.

How the farm works

From farm to you

The journey of every harvest

Simple, careful steps that buyers can see and trust.

01

Grow

Trees are pruned, weeded and mulched by hand through the year, with bananas giving shade between the rows.

02

Pick

Only ripe red coffee cherries and mature banana bunches are harvested, picked by hand.

03

Dry & sort

Coffee is sun-dried on clean tarpaulins and sorted, so stones, sticks and bad cherries stay behind.

04

Deliver

Bagged, weighed in front of you and delivered, or collected at the farm.
