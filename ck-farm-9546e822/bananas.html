Fresh from the garden

How we keep bananas fresh

  • Cut when ready. Bunches are harvested when the fingers are full and rounded.
  • Cut on order day. We do not keep bunches waiting in a store.
  • Handled gently. Carried and loaded with care, so fingers don’t bruise.
  • Grown with care. Mulched with their own leaves, between our coffee rows.

Need banana leaves for steaming matooke or luwombo? Ask, and we will add them to your order.
