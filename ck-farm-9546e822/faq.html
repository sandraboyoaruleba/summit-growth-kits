Coffee

What kind of coffee do you grow?

We grow Robusta, the coffee native to Uganda and the country’s main coffee crop.

What is the difference between kiboko and FAQ?

Kiboko is the whole coffee cherry, dried in the sun. FAQ (Fair Average Quality) is kiboko that has been hulled into green beans. Processors and exporters usually buy FAQ; many traders buy kiboko.

When is coffee available?

Coffee is harvested in seasons. Our main harvests run in the middle and end of the year. Message us to reserve from the next harvest.

How is your coffee priced?

Coffee prices follow the market and change through the season. We confirm today’s price per kg on WhatsApp before you buy, and the bags are weighed in front of you.

What is the smallest coffee order?

From 50 kg of kiboko or 100 kg of FAQ. For smaller amounts, ask us; we can often help.

Bananas

Are bananas available all year?

Yes. Bananas fruit throughout the year, so we can supply regular orders.

How fresh are the bunches?

We cut bunches on the day of your order, so they reach you fresh and firm.

Can I set up a weekly order?

Yes. Restaurants, schools and hotels can set a standing order for a fixed day each week at a price agreed for the month. See standing orders.

Orders, payment & delivery

How do I order?

Use the order form or send a WhatsApp message to +256 752 557 746 with the product, quantity, place and date.

Do you deliver?

We can arrange delivery for larger orders, quoted by distance. Smaller orders can be collected from the farm.

How can I pay?

Mobile Money, bank transfer or cash on collection. The total is agreed before you pay.

Can I buy for export?

We sell to Ugandan buyers and processors who handle export. Talk to us about volumes for the next harvest.

Visiting the farm

Can I visit the farm?

Buyers and partners are welcome to visit by appointment. Send us a WhatsApp message to arrange a day.

Where is the farm?

The farm is in Uganda. We share a map pin and directions on WhatsApp when you book a visit.
