About CK Farm

Rooted in the soil, run by family

CK Farm is a family farm in Uganda led by Cornelia Asiimwe. Six years ago it began with a plan to grow coffee and bananas together. Today eight acres are in production, worked by a team of six to ten people.

  • 8 acres

Our story

Six years of patient work

CK Farm is led by Cornelia Asiimwe. What started six years ago has grown into eight productive acres of coffee and bananas, worked by a dedicated team of six to ten people.

We believe good produce starts with healthy soil and honest work. Every coffee tree is pruned and tended by hand, and our bananas grow between the rows, shading the young coffee and feeding the soil as their leaves fall.

Coffee follows its seasons; bananas fruit all year. Growing both gives the farm, and the people who work on it, a steadier year, and it means buyers can come back to us again and again.

Our promise: produce we would proudly serve at our own table, grown in a way that keeps the land healthy for the next generation.

Meet the farmer

Cornelia Asiimwe

  • Founder
  • Farm owner
  • Meets every buyer
“I want every buyer to know exactly where their coffee and matooke come from: our soil, our hands, our care. That is how you build trust that lasts longer than one season.”Cornelia Asiimwe

Cornelia oversees the farm from planting to sale. She agrees prices with buyers, plans the harvests and keeps an eye on quality, so the coffee is well dried and the bunches are full before anything leaves the farm.

She started CK Farm to build something lasting on the land: a farm that feeds families, pays its workers fairly and grows a little stronger every season.

What we stand for

The team

Six to ten people, one farm

The farm is worked by a team of six to ten people, more at harvest time. Together they prune and weed the coffee, cut and carry banana bunches, spread and turn the drying coffee, and load orders for buyers.

  • A farm lead who plans the work each week with Cornelia
  • Pickers who know when a cherry is ripe and a bunch is ready
  • Drying and sorting at harvest time
  • Loading and delivery on order days

Every bag and every bunch carries the CK Farm name, and Cornelia stands behind it.
