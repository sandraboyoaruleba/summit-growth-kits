Quality

What makes a good bag of coffee

Buyers judge coffee on moisture, cleanliness and defects. These are the habits we keep so our coffee passes.

  • Ripe picking. Red cherries only, so the beans are full and sweet.
  • Clean drying. On tarpaulins, never on bare earth, turned through the day.
  • Properly dry. Dried until the beans rattle, so they store without mould.
  • Sorted by hand. Stones, sticks and black cherries picked out.
  • Stored off the floor. Bagged in clean sacks, in a dry store.
