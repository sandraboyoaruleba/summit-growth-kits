Questions

Before you connect

The questions island households and businesses ask most.

All questionsAsk on WhatsApp
Who can connect?

Homes, businesses and industries within reach of the Bussi mini-grid network. Send us your village and we’ll confirm coverage.

Single phase or three phase?

Single phase suits homes and most shops. Three phase is for heavier machines such as mills, welders and cold rooms. Our team will advise during inspection.

What is a “pole service”?

If your premises are next to the network you may need no new pole (UGX 470,000). If a new pole is needed to reach you, the one-pole service applies (UGX 2,800,000).
