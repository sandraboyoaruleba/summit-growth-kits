Procedure for connection

Getting connected in 5 steps

Clear steps, published fees and a checklist of documents, so your application goes through first time.

Start your applicationAsk a question

The procedure

From application to lights on

  1. 1

    Apply

    Fill in the application form and attach the required documents.

  2. 2

    Pay inspection

    Pay the inspection charge when you submit the form: UGX 38,000 single phase, UGX 80,000 three phase.

  3. 3

    Site inspection

    A Solargen surveyor visits your premises to inspect the site.

  4. 4

    Pay connection fee

    After a successful inspection, pay the connection fee.

  5. 5

    Lights on!

    The connection is made once the connection fee is received.

Step 1 checklist

Documents to attach to your application

Bring these with your application form. Missing documents are the most common reason for delays.

  • Wiring certificatefrom a certified wireman
  • One passport photoof the applicant
  • Copy of national IDboth sides
  • Letter from your village chairmanproving land ownership
  • Wayleave formonly if poles and wires pass through other people’s land
  • Trade licencesole proprietorships
  • Company seal & certificate of incorporationcompanies

Fees

Inspection charges are paid when you submit the form. Connection fees are paid after a successful inspection.

Inspection · single phaseUGX 38,000≈ US$10
Inspection · three phaseUGX 80,000≈ US$22
Connection · no-pole serviceUGX 470,000≈ US$127
Connection · one-pole serviceUGX 2,800,000≈ US$760

Prices in Ugandan shillings (UGX). Dollar amounts are approximate. Please confirm current charges with our office before paying.

What is a pole service?

If your premises are next to the network you may need no new pole. If a new pole is needed to reach you, the one-pole service applies. The surveyor confirms this at inspection.

What to expect

The site inspection

  • A surveyor visits your premises to check your wiring and the distance to the network.
  • We confirm the phase (single or three) and whether a pole is needed.
  • You get the connection fee before any work begins.
  • Safety comes first. We only connect premises wired by a certified wireman.

Ask our office how long each step currently takes in your village.

Ready to apply?

Send your name, village and the type of connection. We’ll tell you exactly what to bring.
