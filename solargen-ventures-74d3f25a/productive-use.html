Business ideas

What could you power?

Productive use means using electricity to make or sell more. These are the businesses that grow fastest on a mini-grid.

Fish cold storage & ice

Keep the catch fresh and sell at better prices, instead of selling cheap at the landing site.

Freezers · cold rooms · ice machines

Milling & processing

Grain mills, feed processing and juice pressing on three-phase power, without diesel runs to the mainland.

Maize mills · feed mixers · juice presses

Welding & workshops

Three-phase power for welders, grinders and drills. Steadier work, cheaper than a generator.

Welders · grinders · boat repair

Salons & tailoring

Clippers, dryers and sewing machines that run all day and into the evening.

Barbers · hair salons · tailors

Shops, cafés & bars

Fridges, music and lights into the night. Cold drinks sell for more.

Fridges · lights · phone charging

Water pumping & irrigation

Pump water for farms and households, and grow more through the dry season.

Pumps · irrigation · water kiosks

Generator vs. grid

Why business owners switch

  • No fuel runs. No buying petrol or diesel every day, no carrying jerrycans across the lake.
  • Longer hours. Power is there when the customers are, including after dark.
  • Bigger machines. Three-phase power runs mills, welders and cold rooms a small generator can’t.
  • Quieter and cleaner. No fumes in the shop, no noise for the neighbours.
