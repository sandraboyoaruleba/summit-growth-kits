With: PATH

CurrentJan – Dec 2025

Galvanizing Collective Action for Adolescent Health

Strengthening district adolescent health committees, tracking FP2030 commitments and mobilising communities around adolescent and school health.

With: Planned Parenthood Global

FP/SRH Budget Analysis Study

Tracking government allocations and spending on family planning and sexual and reproductive health, with recommendations for sustained financing.
