Health tips

From our pharmacists

Finish your course

Always complete antibiotics as prescribed, even when you feel better.

Store medicines well

Keep medicines cool, dry and out of children’s reach, and check expiry dates.

Plan your family

Ask us confidentially about modern family planning options that suit you.

ORS saves lives

For diarrhoea in children, give ORS and zinc early, and seek care if it continues.
