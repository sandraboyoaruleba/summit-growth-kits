01 — Who we are

Locally rooted. Globally trusted.

Incorporated in Uganda in 2012, Samasha is a Global South non-governmental organisation that builds local and technical capacity to deliver internationally rated consulting services and projects.

We support public, private and civil society actors to create synergy and make the most of increasingly scarce resources for the health needs of populations in the Global South. Our technical team brings years of experience in RMNCAH-N, from reproductive health commodity security and health workforce, to health financing, resource tracking, and health technologies.

Vision

A Global South with resilient health systems that accelerate equitable population health and sustainable development.

Mission

To strengthen health systems with a focus on RMNCAH-N through partnerships, evidence-driven policies, capacity strengthening and scalable innovations.

02 — What we do

Three pillars for stronger health systems

Advocacy & Accountability

Multi-stakeholder accountability frameworks that hold family planning and maternal & child health commitment-makers to account, with evidence-based policy advocacy and resource tracking.

Health Systems Strengthening

Building capacities and skills in public institutions and civil society across leadership and governance, access to medicines, health financing, service delivery, workforce and information systems.

Health Innovations & Technology

Generating evidence and scaling innovations, such as digital tools and Total Market Approaches, that help family planning goods and services reach people sustainably and cost-effectively.
