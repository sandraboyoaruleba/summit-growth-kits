Visiting the station

What are your opening hours?

We open early and close late, every day: Daily · 6:00am – 10:00pm. Call us to confirm today’s hours.

Where exactly are you?

In Komamboga, Kawempe Division, Kampala. See the map and directions.

Can I pay with Mobile Money?

Yes. We accept cash and Mobile Money for both fuel and groceries.

Fuel

What are today’s pump prices?

Pump prices change with the market. Call or WhatsApp us for today’s price; the board on our home page shows sample prices.

Can I buy a small amount of fuel?

Yes. Boda riders and drivers can buy from UGX 5,000 or by the litre.

Do you sell fuel in jerrycans?

Yes, in approved containers, for generators and machinery.

Do you sell paraffin and engine oil?

Yes: paraffin for homes and lamps, plus engine oils and lubricants. Ask the attendant.

Do you refill cooking gas?

Ask us on WhatsApp for the cylinder sizes we refill and today’s prices.

Grocery shop & orders

How does ordering on WhatsApp work?

Send your shopping list using the order form or on WhatsApp. We confirm the total and have your bag ready when you arrive.

Do you deliver groceries?

Local delivery around Komamboga may be available. Just ask.

Can I buy airtime and use Mobile Money in the shop?

Yes, airtime and data bundles are sold at the counter, and Mobile Money services are available.

Accounts

Can businesses open a fuel account?

Talk to us about fuel arrangements for boda groups, taxis and company vehicles. See fuel accounts.

How do we settle an account?

Weekly or monthly by Mobile Money or bank transfer, after you receive a statement.
