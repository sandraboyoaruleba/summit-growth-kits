A day in the life

What a day at Armor looks like

A sample of a typical school day. Exact times vary by class.

  1. 7:30

    Warm welcome

    Teachers greet every child at the gate by name.

  2. 8:00

    Morning devotion

    Worship, a Bible thought and prayer for the day.

  3. 8:30

    Core lessons

    Literacy, maths and science while minds are fresh.

  4. 10:30

    Break & play

    A healthy snack, games and friendships.

  5. 11:00

    Explore & create

    Projects, art, music, languages and reading time.

  6. 1:00

    Lunch together

    Shared meals and good manners at the table.

  7. 2:00

    Clubs & sport

    Sports, drama, debate and homework support.

  8. 4:30

    Home time

    Handover to parents, with a word about the day when it matters.
