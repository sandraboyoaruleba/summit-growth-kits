Who we are

A school that feels like family

Our name comes from Ephesians 6. We believe children grow strongest when they are protected by love, grounded in truth and confident in who God made them to be. That is the “armor” we put on every learner.

Led by Shirlein Ssekamate, Armor Christian School brings together academic excellence, personal care and Christian values. Small classes let teachers know each child by name, notice when they struggle, and celebrate when they shine.

Counselling services

Every child deserves someone to talk to

Growing up is not always easy. Our counselling service gives children and teenagers a safe, confidential space to talk about friendships, family changes, grief, exam stress, behaviour or faith questions, guided by caring Christian counsellors.

  • One-to-one sessions for learners
  • Guidance for parents and families
  • Career and subject guidance for older students
  • Also available to children from other schools

FAQ

Parents often ask

We teach children from kindergarten (around age 3) up to grade 12.

We are a Christ-centred school and welcome every family who is happy for their child to learn in a Christian environment.

What are the school fees?

Fees depend on the class. Send an enquiry and we will share the current fees structure.

Do you offer transport or boarding?
