Who we are

A school that feels like family

Our name comes from Ephesians 6. We believe children grow strongest when they are protected by love, grounded in truth and confident in who God made them to be.

That is the “armor” we put on every learner. Small classes let teachers know each child by name, notice when they struggle, and celebrate when they shine.
