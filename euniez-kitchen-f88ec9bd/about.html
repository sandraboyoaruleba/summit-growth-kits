Our story · since 2015

Cooked with integrity, served with love.

Eunie’s Kitchen Ltd is a team of motivated professionals who want to see tasty, healthy, organic Ugandan cuisine appreciated at home and around the world. We’ve been cooking, hosting and celebrating with Uganda’s families since 2015.

Who we are

Your home away from home

Dr. Eunice’s idea was simple: to host you in her big kitchen and let you taste her recipe creations.

Our first restaurant opened at Ntinda Complex, opposite St. Luke Church of Uganda, with a juice bar for fresh fruit juices and a daily menu that took its theme from a different part of Uganda. We hosted birthdays, anniversaries, baptisms and friendships, and took our cooking out to weddings and introductions across Kampala, Wakiso and Mukono.

Today we welcome guests at two restaurants, in Mukono and Kasangati, with a kids’ play space, the EK pizzeria and the same promise: the most affordable tasty meals, the friendliest service and the highest standards of hygiene.

The meaning of our tagline

Mind. Body. Soul.

It’s written under our logo because it’s how we were founded: a company that cooks three types of food.

Body

Our founder

Dr. Eunice Adubango

  • Founder & Managing Partner
  • Doctorate in Civil Engineering
  • Former lecturer, Makerere University
  • 12+ years teaching cookery
  • Author, speaker & mother
If you are looking for rich, unique, healthy meals, come and visit with me. I’ll host you in my big kitchen, feed you well, and send you home blessed.Dr. Eunice AdubangoFounder, Eunie’s Kitchen

“With a doctorate in Civil Engineering, Eunice is a natural in the kitchen.” An engineer, teacher and cook, Dr. Eunice built Eunie’s Kitchen around her own recipes and her faith. She still develops our signature dishes, teaches cookery and catering, and shares business wisdom through her “Business Nuggets”.

In 2025 she founded Ziva Flourish Centre in Kasangati: cookery classes, Christian counselling and retreats under one roof.

Our journey

From one big kitchen to two restaurants

A few milestones from our records.

Mukono launch, Oct 2018
  1. 2015

    Eunie’s Kitchen Ltd begins

    Dr. Eunice starts cooking three kinds of food: for the body, the mind and the soul.

  2. 2017

    Celebrations across Kampala

    Weddings, introductions and family reunions fill our catering calendar.

  3. 2018

    Mukono branch opens

    On 9 October 2018 we launch Eunie’s Kitchen Mukono, opposite the District Headquarters.

  4. 2019

    Our biggest wedding, and a culinary school

    330 guests at Pius & Shivan’s wedding at Ndere Centre; our physical culinary school starts in August.

  5. 2020

    Teaching online

    Free Business Nuggets and children’s faith classes reach families at home.

  6. 2025

    Ziva Flourish Centre

    Dr. Eunice opens Ziva Flourish Centre in Kasangati, and our latest branch adds books and play to your favourite meals.
