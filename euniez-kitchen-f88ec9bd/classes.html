What to expect

How a class works

  1. Pick a class & date

    Tell us what you want to learn and when you’re free. Walk-in or booked.

  2. Arrive & apron up

    Ingredients and equipment are ready. Bring a notebook and your appetite.

  3. Cook it yourself

    Our instructor demonstrates, then you do it, with help and tips at every step.

  4. Taste & take home

    Enjoy what you made, take home your notes, and come back for the next skill.

Our teaching story

We’ve been teaching for years

Long before Ziva, the Eunie’s Kitchen office and training space at Ntinda Complex was full of aprons, notebooks and flour.

Our cake for a training day
  1. 2018

    Snack and cooking classes

    Hands-on June snack class and cooking classes at our Ntinda training space.

  2. 2019

    The culinary school starts

    A February–March cooking intake, our breakfast items class, an ice cream making class, and a physical culinary school from 3 August 2019.

  3. 2020

    High-level cake decoration, and classes online

    Advanced bakery classes, plus free online “Business Nuggets” for entrepreneurs and faith classes teaching children to journey with God through conversation.

  4. 2025

    A new home at Ziva Flourish Centre

    Dr. Eunice opens Ziva Flourish Centre in Kasangati, with walk-in cookery, bakery, kids’ holiday and one-on-one classes.
