How it works

From first message to last plate

  1. Tell us about the day

    Send the date, venue, type of event and roughly how many guests, on WhatsApp or the form.

  2. Get menu options

    We suggest menus that fit your guests and budget, and adjust until it feels right.

  3. Confirm the details

    Agree the final menu, numbers and timings. We plan the cooking, transport and set-up.

  4. We cook, serve & clear

    Our team arrives early, sets the buffet, serves your guests with care and leaves the space tidy.

Book as early as you can for weddings and introductions. Deposit, minimum numbers and travel outside Kampala, Mukono and Wakiso are confirmed with your quote.
