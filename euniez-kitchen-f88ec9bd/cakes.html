How to order

Your cake in four easy steps

  1. Share the occasion

    Date, number of guests, and pick-up branch or delivery address.

  2. Choose a design

    Send a photo idea, colours, the verse or message, and your flavour.

  3. Confirm the price

    We confirm the size, price and collection time on WhatsApp before we bake.

  4. Collect & celebrate

    Pick up from Mukono or Kasangati, or ask us to deliver with your catering.

Please give us a few days’ notice for custom and tiered cakes.
