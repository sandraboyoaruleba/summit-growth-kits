Mind · Body · Soul

We cook three kinds of food

Since 2015, Eunie’s Kitchen has served food for the body, food for the mind and food for the soul. Our name and our tagline are one promise: you leave fuller than you came, in every way.

01

Body

Ugandan cuisine with signature recipes

Local favourites like luwombo, matooke and goat choma, plus pizza, grills, pastries and juices pressed at our own juice bar.

02

Mind

Classes, books & business nuggets

Cookery and bakery classes, catering training for the corporate woman and the youth, and inspirational books by Dr. Eunice.

03

Soul

A place to celebrate life’s moments

Inspirational music, a God-honouring welcome, and tables for birthdays, baptisms, anniversaries and friendships.

Meet our founder

Dr. Eunice Adubango

  • Founder & Managing Partner
  • Doctorate in Civil Engineering
  • 12+ years teaching cookery
I wanted a place where you feel hosted in my own big kitchen: where you taste my recipe creations, celebrate the people you love, and go home fed in body, mind and soul.Dr. Eunice AdubangoFounder, Eunie’s Kitchen & Ziva Flourish Centre

An engineer by training and a cook at heart, Dr. Eunice started Eunie’s Kitchen in 2015 to celebrate the rich, tasty cuisines of Uganda. She still writes our signature recipes, teaches, and in 2025 opened Ziva Flourish Centre in Kasangati.
