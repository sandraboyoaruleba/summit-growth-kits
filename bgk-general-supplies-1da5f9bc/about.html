Our story

Clean, honestly weighed, on time.

BGK General Supplies is a small Ugandan supplier of dry produce and cereals, founded by Bahimbisomwe Gladys, serving hotels, schools, restaurants and homes.

How it started

A store cupboard for other people’s kitchens

Every kitchen in Uganda runs on the same few sacks: beans, posho, rice, millet and g-nuts. When those sacks are dirty, short or late, the whole kitchen suffers.

Gladys started BGK General Supplies to be the supplier she would want to buy from: produce that is sorted and clean, weighed honestly in front of the customer, and delivered when promised. She began by supplying families and small eateries, and grew into wholesale as schools and hotels asked for regular, larger orders.

Today BGK sells both retail and wholesale, from a single kilo for a family to bulk sacks for an institution, with a small team of two to five people who handle sorting, weighing, orders and deliveries.

What the stamp means

Sorted. Weighed. Delivered.

Three words on our stamp, the three things we check on every order, and one promise on price.

Sorted

Stones, chaff, broken grains and weevils are removed before produce reaches the shelf.

Weighed

Every order is weighed on a scale you can see. Receipts on request for institutions.

Delivered

Pick up from the store, or we bring it to your kitchen, school or home.

Fair

Today’s market price, confirmed before you pay. Wholesale rates for bulk.

From sack to store

Five checks before anything leaves

  1. 1

    Buy dry, buy clean

    We buy from trusted traders and farmers and check moisture by hand and eye.

  2. 2

    Sort and sieve

    Stones, husks and broken grains come out before produce is shelved.

  3. 3

    Store off the floor

    Sacks sit on pallets in a dry store, away from damp and pests.

  1. 4

    Weigh in front of you

    Your order is weighed on the scale, and the weight is written on the sack or receipt.

  2. 5

    Pack to keep

    Clean bags in sizes that suit you, tied tight for the trip home or to school.
