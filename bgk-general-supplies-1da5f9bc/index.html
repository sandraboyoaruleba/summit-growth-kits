Our story

Good food starts with a good store cupboard.

BGK General Supplies was started by Bahimbisomwe Gladys with a simple promise: the beans, flour and grains you buy should be clean, honestly weighed and on time. Today a small, dedicated team supplies dry produce and cereals both retail and wholesale.

Whether you are a school bursar planning a term of meals, a hotel chef restocking for the weekend, or a family doing the monthly shopping, we treat every order the same way: sorted, weighed in front of you and packed to keep.

Questions

Good to know

Do you sell small quantities?

Yes. We sell retail from a single kilo and wholesale in sacks and bulk, so homes and institutions are both welcome.

Do you deliver?

Yes, we arrange delivery for orders within Kampala and nearby areas; a minimum order may apply. Ask us on WhatsApp for your location.

Can schools and hotels get a standing order?

Absolutely. We can plan a weekly, monthly or termly supply schedule so your store is always topped up.

Why don’t you list fixed prices?

Dry produce prices change with harvests and the season. To be fair to you, we confirm the day’s price before every order.

How do I pay?

Mobile Money, bank transfer or cash on delivery. Institutions can request an invoice.
