Our story

Good food starts with a good store cupboard.

BGK General Supplies was started by Bahimbisomwe Gladys with a simple promise: the beans, flour and grains you buy should be clean, honestly weighed and on time.

Today a small, dedicated team supplies dry produce and cereals, retail and wholesale. Whether you are a school bursar planning a term of meals, a hotel chef restocking for the weekend, or a family doing the monthly shopping, every order is treated the same way: sorted, weighed in front of you and packed to keep.

Why BGK

Small team. Big standards.

  • Sorted and clean. Every batch is checked for stones, chaff and weevils before it leaves.
  • Honest weight. Weighed in front of you, receipts on request.
  • Retail or wholesale. One kilo or a hundred sacks, you deal with the same people.
  • Reliable supply. Standing orders for institutions, so you never run out mid-term.
  • Easy ordering. Message us on WhatsApp, get a quote, confirm, done.
