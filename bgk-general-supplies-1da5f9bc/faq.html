Ordering

Do you sell small quantities?

Yes. We sell retail from a single kilo and wholesale in sacks and bulk loads, so homes and institutions are both welcome.

How do I place an order?

Use the order builder, send a list on WhatsApp to +256 757 925 917, or call us. We confirm prices and a total before you pay.

Why don’t you list fixed prices?

Dry produce prices change with harvests and the season. To be fair to you, we confirm the day’s price before every order. The prices on this site are a guide.

Can I see the produce before I buy?

Yes. Visit the store during opening hours and we will weigh in front of you. We share the exact location on WhatsApp.

Delivery & payment

Do you deliver?

Yes, within Kampala and nearby areas. Delivery fees depend on distance and order size, and a minimum order may apply. Ask us on WhatsApp for your location.

How soon can you deliver?

Orders confirmed in the morning can often be delivered the same day within Kampala. Bulk and upcountry orders are scheduled.

How do I pay?

Mobile Money, bank transfer or cash on delivery. Institutions can request an invoice.

Do I get a receipt?

Yes. We give receipts and delivery notes on request, which schools and hotels use for their records.

Schools, hotels & wholesale

Can schools and hotels get a standing order?

Yes. We plan a weekly, monthly or termly supply schedule so your store is always topped up. See wholesale plans.

How do you work out quantities for a school?

Tell us your enrolment, how many meals you serve each day and the number of weeks in the term. We suggest quantities and split them into deliveries.

Do you give wholesale prices?

Yes. Wholesale rates apply to sacks and bulk orders. We confirm them on the day of the order.

Quality

How do you keep produce clean?

Every batch is sorted and sieved to remove stones, chaff and broken grains, and stored in sacks off the floor in a dry store.

What if something is wrong with my order?

Tell us on WhatsApp the same day, with a photo. If the produce is not as promised, we will replace it.
