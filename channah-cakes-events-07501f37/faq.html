Ordering

How early should I order?

Plain tea cakes and cookies: 1–2 days. Custom designed cakes: 3–5 days. Wedding cakes: 2–4 weeks, so we can plan the design together. Last minute? Message us and we’ll tell you honestly what we can do.

How do I place an order?

The quickest way is WhatsApp on +254 721 868 212, or the order form on this site, which opens WhatsApp with your details already typed.

Can you copy a cake from a photo?

Yes. Send the picture and we’ll recreate it or add our own twist, and tell you if any detail changes the price.

Can I change my order after I’ve paid the deposit?

Small changes (message, colours) are fine up to 2 days before. Bigger changes, like size or design, depend on what’s already been bought or baked. We’ll always tell you first.

Cakes & flavours

How big a cake do I need?

As a guide, 1 kg serves about 10 people. A 2 kg cake serves about 20, and 3 kg about 30. For 50+ guests we suggest tiers or a display cake with extra cutting cake. See sizes & servings.

Which flavours do you bake?

Vanilla, chocolate, red velvet, lemon, fruit cake, and black or white forest. Ask about other flavours.

Do you make plain cakes as well as designed ones?

Yes. Plain tea cakes start from KES 600 (≈ US$5), and are perfect for the office, visitors or the family table.

Can you bake for allergies or eggless?

Tell us about any allergy before you order. We’ll be honest about what we can safely bake in our kitchen.

Prices & payment

How much does a custom cake cost?

Designed cakes start from KES 2,500 per kg (≈ US$19), and birthday cakes from KES 1,800 (≈ US$14). Tiers, sugar flowers and toppers add to the price. You always get a clear quote first.

How do I pay?

M-Pesa or cash. A deposit secures your date, and the balance is paid on delivery or pick-up.

Do you supply juice and cookies for events?

Absolutely. Juice by the bottle or litre and cookie boxes alongside your cake, in one easy order. See event packages.

Delivery & pick-up

Do you deliver?

Yes. We deliver within Nairobi and nearby areas for a fee that depends on distance. Pick-up is also available.

How should I carry a tall cake?

Keep the box flat on the floor of the car (not on a seat), drive gently and keep it out of the sun. Store cream cakes in a cool place until cutting.
