Our story

A small kitchen with a big love for celebrations.

Channah Cakes & Events was started by Caroline Munyi with a simple idea: everyone deserves a cake that feels made just for them. Some people want a towering, fully designed showpiece. Others just want a beautiful plain cake for Sunday tea. We love baking both.

Alongside our cakes, we bake cookies and press fresh ready juice, so we can take care of the sweet side of your whole event, from the first guest to the last slice.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

How early should I order?

Plain cakes and cookies: 1–2 days. Custom designed cakes: 3–5 days. Wedding cakes: 2–4 weeks so we can plan tastings and design together.

How big a cake do I need?

As a guide, 1 kg serves about 10 people. Tell us your guest count and we'll recommend the right size.

Do you deliver?

Yes, we deliver within Nairobi and nearby areas for a small fee depending on distance. Pick-up is also available.

Can you match a photo I found online?

Yes! Send us the picture and we'll recreate it or add our own twist, and let you know if any details change the price.

Do you supply juice and cookies for events?

Absolutely. We can supply juice by the bottle or litre and cookie boxes alongside your cake for one easy order.
