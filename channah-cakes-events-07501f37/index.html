Questions

Good to know

The questions customers ask us most. Can’t find yours?

All questionsAsk on WhatsApp
How early should I order?

Plain cakes and cookies: 1–2 days. Custom designed cakes: 3–5 days. Wedding cakes: 2–4 weeks, so we can plan the design and tasting together.

How big a cake do I need?

As a guide, 1 kg serves about 10 people. Tell us your guest count and we’ll recommend the right size.

Do you deliver?

Yes. We deliver within Nairobi and nearby areas for a fee that depends on distance, and pick-up is also available.

Can you copy a cake from a photo?

Yes. Send us the picture and we’ll recreate it or add our own twist, and tell you if any detail changes the price.
