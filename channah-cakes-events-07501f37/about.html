Our story

A small kitchen with a big love for celebrations

Channah Cakes & Events is Caroline Munyi’s home of custom cakes, plain tea cakes, cookies and ready fresh juice, baked to order in Kenya.

See our cakesTalk to Caroline

How it started

Everyone deserves a cake that feels made just for them

Some people want a towering, fully designed showpiece. Others just want a beautiful plain cake for Sunday tea. We love baking both.

Caroline started baking for family and friends: birthday cakes for the children, tea cakes for visitors, cookies for church functions. Word spread, orders grew, and Channah Cakes & Events was born.

Alongside cakes, we bake cookies and press fresh ready juice, so we can take care of the sweet side of your whole event, from the first guest to the last slice.

Meet the founder

Caroline Munyi

“When you order from me, you’re not buying a cake off a shelf. I want to hear about your day, your colours and your people, and then bake something that fits.”Caroline Munyi, founder

Caroline bakes every order herself and talks every customer through size, flavour and design before a single egg is cracked. She answers on WhatsApp, sends a clear price, and keeps you updated until the cake is in your hands.

The name

Why “Channah”?

Channah is the Hebrew form of the name Hannah, which means grace or favour. It is a fitting name for a kitchen that bakes for life’s blessings: weddings, new babies, graduations and birthdays.

Please note: we are Channah Cakes & Events, Caroline Munyi’s bakery. We are not linked to any similarly named business.

In the kitchen

What goes into every bake

  • Fresh ingredients. Real butter, fresh eggs and good flour, bought for your order.
  • Baked close to your date. So your cake is soft and moist when it is cut.
  • Hygiene first. A clean kitchen, clean hands and food-safe boxes for every order.
  • Packed with care. Boxed and steadied for the journey, with handling tips for tall cakes.
