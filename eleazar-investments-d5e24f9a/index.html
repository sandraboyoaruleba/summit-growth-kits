The meaning behind the name

Eleazar means “God has helped.”

It is a reminder that every season of growth is built on grace, wisdom and the courage to begin.

Founded by Juliet Mulungi, who lives between the United States and Uganda, Eleazar Investments is a home for practical, well-designed digital resources. They distil hard-won lessons about money, business and personal growth into tools you can use today.

No fluff and no overwhelm: just clear frameworks, elegant templates and honest guidance for people building something meaningful, wherever in the world they are.
