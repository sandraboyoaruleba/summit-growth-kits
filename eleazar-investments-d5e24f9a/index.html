The meaning behind the name

Eleazar means “God has helped.”

It is a reminder that every season of growth is built on grace, wisdom and the courage to begin.

Founded by Juliet Mulungi, who lives between the United States and Uganda, Eleazar Investments is a home for practical, well-designed digital resources. They distil hard-won lessons about money, business and personal growth into tools you can use today.

The questions people ask before launch.

All questions
When do the products launch?

The first collection is in its final stages. Join the waitlist and you’ll hear first, with founding-member pricing.

Are these physical or digital?

Everything is digital: downloadable guides, printable planners, editable templates and online courses you can use anywhere in the world.

Can I pay from Uganda?

Yes, that is the plan. Checkout is being set up for customers in both the USA and East Africa: card payments, and mobile money options for Uganda. Every price shows US dollars with an approximate amount in Ugandan shillings.

Is this financial advice?

No. Our resources are educational and designed to build good habits and understanding. For decisions about your own situation, please speak to a licensed professional.
