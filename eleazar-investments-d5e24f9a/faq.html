The products

When do the products launch?

The first collection is in its final stages. Join the waitlist and you’ll hear first, with founding-member pricing.

Are these physical or digital?

Everything is digital: downloadable guides, printable planners, editable templates and online courses you can use anywhere in the world.

How do I receive my purchase?

Straight after checkout you receive a download link (and course login where relevant) by email. You can download again at any time.

Can I print the planners?

Yes. Planners and worksheets come as printable PDFs (A4 and US Letter) and, where noted, as a digital version for tablet note-taking apps.

Are there free resources?

Yes. Free starter resources will be shared with the waitlist first. See free resources.

Paying & prices

Why are prices shown in two currencies?

Because Eleazar serves people in both the USA and Uganda. Prices are set in US dollars; the Ugandan shilling amounts are approximate (US$1 ≈ UGX 3,700) and the exact amount depends on your payment method on the day.

Can I pay from Uganda?

Yes, that is the plan. Checkout is being set up for customers in both the USA and East Africa: card payments, and mobile money options for Uganda. Every price shows US dollars with an approximate amount in Ugandan shillings.

What if it isn’t right for me?

Because products are digital, refunds are handled case by case. If something doesn’t work for you, message Juliet and she will make it right.

Can I buy for a group, church or team?

Yes. Group and organisation licences will be available. Get in touch with the number of people and Juliet will send a quote.

Is this financial advice?

No. Our resources are educational and designed to build good habits and understanding. For decisions about your own situation, please speak to a licensed professional.

Staying in touch

Can I suggest a product?

Please do. The waitlist form asks what you’d love help with, and those answers shape what Juliet creates next.

Where can I follow along?

Instagram, TikTok, Facebook and YouTube accounts are being set up. Links will appear on the journal page.

What do you do with my details?

Your details are only used to send you launch news and resources you asked for. They are never sold or shared, and you can ask to be removed at any time.
