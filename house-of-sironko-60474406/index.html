Our story

A house built on honest food.

House of Sironko is led by Stella Wadulo and a small, hands-on team who care for every stage, from feeding and raising our birds to the moment an order reaches your door.

Our name carries our roots in Sironko, in the green foothills of Mount Elgon, a place where good food is shared generously. That spirit is in every tray and every bird we sell.

  • Well fedScheduled, balanced feeding for healthy, meaty birds.
  • Handled cleanHygienic preparation and careful packing.
  • Always freshSold fresh, never left sitting for days.

Yes, we deliver within Kampala and nearby areas. Delivery fees depend on your location and order size.

Just tell us how you'd like it when you order, whole or cut, and we'll prepare it for you.

Yes, we welcome regular weekly or monthly supply arrangements. Ask us for a bulk quote.
