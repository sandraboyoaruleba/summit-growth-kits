Sizes & fit

How do I know my size?

Most adults know their EU size (for example 38 or 42). If you don’t, measure your foot at home with our size guide and send us the length in centimetres on WhatsApp. We’ll tell you which size to take in the style you like, because sizes differ a little between makers.

Can I try shoes on before I pay?

Yes. Come to the shop and walk around in them; take your time. If you order on WhatsApp first, we can keep your pair aside so it’s ready when you arrive.

What sizes do you stock for children?

From small children’s sizes (around EU 22) up to teen sizes (EU 40 and above). School shoes go fast in December and January, so send your child’s size early.

Do you have wide fittings or big sizes?

Some styles come in bigger sizes (EU 46 and up) and roomier fittings. Tell us your size on WhatsApp and we’ll send photos of what fits.

Ordering, paying & delivery

How do I order?

Tap “Order” on any style, or use the order form. Your message opens in WhatsApp; we reply with real photos of the pair, the price and your size, and you confirm.

How can I pay?

M-Pesa or cash in the shop. We confirm the total on WhatsApp before you pay.

Do you deliver?

Yes, we can arrange delivery. The fee depends on where you are; ask us on WhatsApp and we’ll quote before you pay.

Can I reserve a pair?

Yes. Send the style and size and we’ll keep it aside for you for an agreed time.

I live abroad. Can I buy shoes for my family in Kenya?

Yes. Send us the sizes and a budget, we send photos, and the shoes can be collected from the shop or delivered to your family. We’ll agree the easiest way to pay. Prices are in Kenyan shillings; dollar amounts on this site are approximate.

Exchanges, quality & care

What if the size is wrong?

Bring them back unworn within a few days with your receipt and we’ll exchange the size if we have it.

Are your shoes genuine leather?

Some are leather and some are leather-look or synthetic; we always tell you which before you buy. Ask about any pair and we’ll be honest about the material.

How do I make my shoes last?

Let them dry naturally, polish leather regularly and rotate pairs. Our care guide has simple tips for leather, suede, sneakers and school shoes.

Do you sell in bulk to schools or churches?

Yes, ask us about group orders: send the sizes and quantities on WhatsApp and we’ll come back with a quote.
