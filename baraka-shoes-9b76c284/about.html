Our story

A shop called blessing

Baraka is Swahili for “blessing”. That’s what a good pair of shoes should feel like, and what a fair price feels like to the family paying for it.

Kenya

In her own words

“I sell footwear that are affordable, quality and comfortable.”

Rahab Wangui Gachigi, describing Baraka Shoes Shop

Three promises, every pair

Baraka Shoes Shop is a family-sized business run by Rahab Wangui Gachigi with a team of two to five people. It sells shoes for men, women and children, chosen for three things that matter to the people who buy them.

Rahab started the shop to give families in her area good shoes at fair prices: school shoes before January, official shoes for work and church, and comfortable everyday pairs. (Story to be confirmed with Rahab.)

The name

Why “Baraka”?

Baraka is the Swahili word for blessing. We like to think every pair that leaves the shop carries a little of it: to the child who walks to school in comfort, the mother who didn’t overpay, the young man who looks sharp at his first interview.

Ask Rahab and she’ll tell you the rest of the story.
