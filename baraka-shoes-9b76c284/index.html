Before you buy

Sizes, payment, delivery and exchanges.

All questions →
How do I know my size?

Most adults know their EU size (for example 38 or 42). If you don’t, measure your foot at home with our size guide and send us the length in centimetres on WhatsApp. We’ll tell you which size to take in the style you like, because sizes differ a little between makers.

How can I pay?

M-Pesa or cash in the shop. We confirm the total on WhatsApp before you pay.

Do you deliver?

Yes, we can arrange delivery. The fee depends on where you are; ask us on WhatsApp and we’ll quote before you pay.

What if the size is wrong?

Bring them back unworn within a few days with your receipt and we’ll exchange the size if we have it.
