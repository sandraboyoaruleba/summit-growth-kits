Measure at home

Get the size right first time

Children’s feet can grow a size in a few months. Measure just before you order.

  1. 1

    Paper on the floor

    Put a sheet of paper flat against a wall. Your child stands on it, heel touching the wall, in school socks.

  2. 2

    Mark the longest toe

    Mark the tip of the longest toe with a pencil held straight up. Do both feet; use the longer one.

  3. 3

    Measure in cm

    Measure from the wall to the mark. Send us that number on WhatsApp.

  4. 4

    We add growing room

    We suggest a size with about a thumb’s width (1–1.5 cm) of space, enough to grow, not enough to trip.

A quick guide. Makers differ by up to half a size, so we always check against the actual pair before you buy.

  • Measure late in the day, when feet are largest
  • Measure in the socks they’ll wear to school
  • Check the school’s rules on colour and style
