Our care

Support for every stage of the journey

Every family is different. We match the level of care and the hours of service to what you and your baby need.

Expectant mothers

Antenatal support at home in the final weeks: wellbeing checks, rest and nutrition guidance, and preparing your home and heart for baby’s arrival.

Postnatal mother care

Gentle recovery support after birth, including after a C-section: wound and comfort care, rest, emotional reassurance and help with daily routines.

Newborn care

Bathing, cord care, feeding, burping, soothing and safe sleep, guided by a professional so you learn with confidence.

Night nursing

A trained nurse cares for baby through the night and brings them to you for feeds, so the whole household can finally sleep.

Breastfeeding support

Hands-on help with latching, positioning and expressing, plus practical advice that eases the worry of the early days.

Infant care

Continued care as your baby grows: routines, development milestones and a trusted pair of hands when you return to work.

About JoyfulHome

Care that feels like family, with a nurse’s skill

HomeCare by JoyfulHome offers high-quality home nursing care for expectant and nursing mothers and for newborns and infants. Led by Rachael Mugenyi, our small, dedicated team of 6–10 people brings professional care into the comfort of your own home.

We believe a calm, supported mother raises a thriving baby. That’s why our care is personal: we listen first, then build a plan around your family, your routines and your budget.

  • Qualified, registered nurses and midwives
  • Care plans tailored to your needs and hours
  • Respectful, discreet and warm in your home
  • Serving families across Kampala & surrounding areas

Questions

Good to know

Can’t find your answer? Ask us on WhatsApp and we’ll reply.

How much does home care cost?

Our rates depend on the level of care and the hours of service you need. Share your situation with us and we’ll send a tailored quote with no obligation.

When should I book?

Ideally a few weeks before your due date so we can plan together, but we also help families who need support at short notice after birth.

Can you care for twins or premature babies?

Yes, talk to us about your baby’s needs and we’ll put together the right level of care and support for your family.

Do you only care for the baby?

No. We care for mothers too: during pregnancy, after birth and while breastfeeding. A healthy, rested mother is at the heart of everything we do.

Which areas do you cover?

We serve families in Kampala and nearby areas. Message us with your location to confirm.
