Our story

A royal welcome, every single visit.

The Royal Lounge is a restaurant, café and grill in Lubowa where Uganda’s best-loved dishes sit proudly beside international favourites. Whether it’s a slow Sunday luwombo, a sizzling mixed grill with friends or a quiet cappuccino before work, every plate is prepared fresh and served with genuine warmth.

Can’t make it in? We deliver. Send your order on WhatsApp and we’ll bring the Royal Lounge to your door.

Diane AkansasiraProprietor, The Royal Lounge
